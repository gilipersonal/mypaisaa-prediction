Mypaisaa Prediction · how it works

MyPrediction

A private forecaster for one person's shares, funds and ETFs in the US, the UK and India. Every day it shows what might happen next, how sure it is, and why. Then it checks itself against what really happened, and gets better.

Ranges, never one number Scored after the fact Never trades

Status: planned and designed, not built yet. Only a small test server and the project's checks run today.

What it does for you

Four jobs, done every day

Watches your money

Your holdings and a watchlist, 20 to 30 to start, priced after each market closes and checked every 30 minutes while markets are open.

Forecasts in ranges

Not "it will go up". It says where the price is likely to land and how wide the uncertainty is, for each time ahead below.

Explains in plain words

A 07:30 morning briefing and a chat that answers "why?". Claude writes the words; the numbers come from the maths.

Keeps score and learns

Every forecast is frozen when it is made and marked against reality later. The track record is always on show.

Short ranges help with timing; the 3, 6 and 12-month forecasts are the heart of it, because MyPrediction is built for long-term investing. The 3 and 5-year views are scenarios (good, middle, bad), never predictions.

It never trades. It holds no broker login, places no orders and is not financial advice. It suggests add, hold, pause, trim or rebalance, with reasons; you decide.

Who does what

Four parts, four jobs, no overlap

Code

Does every sum: prices, currencies, values, scores. The same inputs always give the same answer.

Models

Forecast the ranges and the chances, from about ten years of history, events and similar past days.

Claude

Reads the news, tags it, and explains forecasts in plain words. It never makes up a number.

You

Decide everything that matters: what to buy or sell, which new model goes live, and the limits it learns within.

How it gets smarter

It corrects itself, inside limits you set

  1. Every forecast is markedWhen its time is up, each forecast is compared with what really happened: was the real price inside the range, and was it as sure as it should have been?
  2. Small fixes happen by themselvesIf it has been a little over-confident or under-confident, ordinary code nudges its settings, but only within limits you approve in advance. If the next scores get worse, the nudge is undone automatically.
  3. Big changes wait for youA new model, a new kind of signal or anything outside your limits is tested on years of past data, then runs quietly beside the live one, and only goes live when you say yes.
  4. It stops itself if something looks wrongIf a safety alarm you agreed in advance goes off, that forecast pauses and you are told. It restarts only with your yes.

owner-chosen bounded self-correction, 3 November 2026. to set the size of the limits, before launch. Claude never changes a model; only code does, inside the limits.

Promises it keeps

Honest by design

Uncertainty is always shownEvery forecast carries its range, its time ahead, when it was made and how good its record is.
It can say "not sure"When the evidence is thin, it holds back rather than guess.
Stale data is labelledA late price or a provider problem is shown on screen, never hidden behind a spinner.
History is never rewrittenA forecast is frozen the moment it is made, so the track record cannot be flattered.
Your data stays yoursPrivate to the owner, no passwords to brokers, and secrets never in logs or screenshots.
Quiet at nightNo alerts between 22:00 and 07:00; anything that waits arrives with the 07:30 briefing.
