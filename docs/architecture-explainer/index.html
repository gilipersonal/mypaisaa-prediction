In plain words

Four jobs, done every day

If you read nothing else: MyPrediction watches what you own, says where each price is likely to land with honest ranges, explains why in plain words, and keeps score of itself. Everything below is how.

Watches your money

Your holdings and a watchlist, 20 to 30 listings to start, priced after each market closes and checked every 30 minutes while markets are open. A curated list of about 150 more names is scanned at end of day for the top 5 per segment.

Forecasts in ranges

Not "it will go up". Where the price is likely to land and how wide the uncertainty is, for each horizon below.

Explains in plain words

A 07:30 London briefing and a chat that answers "why?". Claude writes the words; every number comes from code and models.

Keeps score and learns

Each forecast is frozen when made and graded after its horizon. Small fixes happen by themselves, inside limits you set.

It never trades. No broker login, no orders, no personalised financial advice. It suggests add, hold, pause, trim or rebalance, with reasons and uncertainty; you decide.

    The forecast engine · ARC-04, ARC-05

    Baselines first, then bounded challengers, eight capabilities kept

    Each target, horizon, currency and market kind is its own task with its own eligibility. Never a random split: walk-forward folds with a purge and embargo sized to the horizon, every transform fitted inside its fold, labels used only once they have matured. The order is Owner-selected; the module specifications are Proposed and nothing is trained.

    R0

    Baselines

    Fixed rules a person can check, zero return, trailing events, empirical distributions and a block bootstrap over 252 and 756 observations.

    To pass: correct semantics, chronological comparison. Every model has to beat it.

    R1

    Regularised

    Ridge for returns, logistic for events, a sigmoid calibration map fitted on its own chronological block.

    To pass: fold-local transforms, purging, matured labels, justified calibration.

    R2

    Challengers

    Histogram gradient-boosted return, quantile and event models, which may take other modules' outputs only when an ablation shows they add value.

    To pass: beat the baseline with uncertainty and coverage, then shadow, then the owner's promotion.

    The three Claude models

    Controlled learning · owner, 3 November 2026

    It corrects itself inside bands, and asks for anything bigger

    After each close, code, never a Claude agent, grades every forecast that has matured and makes small calibration and weighting adjustments inside bands the owner approves in advance. An adjustment that makes scores worse rolls itself back. New models, features and rules, and anything outside a band, wait for the owner, and the POLICY-003 safety pause stays. The limits are moderate: a forecast's probabilities may shift at most 5 percentage points a week, and how much a signal counts may change at most 10% a month; anything larger waits for the owner. A challenger spends 8 weeks in shadow, beating both the base rate and the rules, before it can be promoted with the owner's yes. For the long horizons, the 8 weeks check that a model runs cleanly and is calibrated on what has matured; years of walk-forward history carry the accuracy evidence for the 3, 6 and 12-month horizons, and the owner still approves. A promoted model that scores worse than the previous approved one for 4 weeks running, on matured forecasts, reverts by itself to it. That revert is automatic; restarting a paused forecast, or switching forward, still needs the owner's yes, and POLICY-003 stands. Owner-selected 3 November 2026.

    Graded only after the horizonRefreshing daily never matures a 12-month outcome. For 3, 6 and 12 months the 8-week trial checks clean running and calibration on what has matured; years of walk-forward history carry the accuracy evidence. Scores report hit rate beside calibration, coverage and the benchmark.
    In band, alone; outside, neverThe calibrator is deterministic code. Any change past a band, a new model, feature or rule, waits for the owner.
    Rollback is automaticAn adjustment on probation that worsens scores reverts to the last good version. A promoted model that scores worse than the previous approved one for 4 weeks running, on matured forecasts, reverts to it and tells the owner; switching forward again needs the owner's yes.
    A safety pausePOLICY-003: if an agreed alarm fires, that scope pauses and the owner is told. It restarts only with the owner's yes; only the revert to the previous approved model is automatic.

    It cannot trade

    No broker credentials, no order path, no personalised advice. Suggestions only, with uncertainty on show.

    AGENTS.md · rule 4

    One writer, exact approvals

    Imports become drafts. The controlled writer commits only the exact change the owner approved, within 15 minutes, with its audit row.

    ARC-02 · ARC-07

    Untrusted text stays data

    Files, pages and tool output are never instructions. Extraction runs with no tools and no write authority; source text in a history stays untrusted.

    ARC-06 · ARC-09

    Step-up for what matters

    A promotion, a commit or a restart needs a fresh, strong sign-in bound to that action. A local Face ID alone never authorises anything on the server.

    ARC-07

    Frozen, then graded

    A forecast records its cutoff, versions, horizon and full distribution, and is scored only after the horizon. History is never rewritten.
