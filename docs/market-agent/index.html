US, UK and Indian shares and ETFs from the start, 20 to 30 listings first and a curated discovery list of about 150 names scanned on end-of-day data, for long-term investing. Each forecast is frozen when it is made and graded only after its horizon has passed.

My target is to minimize loss and grow money over time steadily.The owner. Every part below is judged against this goal: fewer deep falls first, steady growth second. The combined score weighs protection 40, growth 35 and opportunity 25, and only when all three have evidence (POLICY-006).

The honest starting point

What can and cannot be forecast

OWNER-SELECTED · seven horizons, POLICY-004 scenariosPROPOSED · PH-04 · L4-S01

Risk and mood can be forecast; tomorrow's up or down mostly cannot. Calm and rough spells cluster, so how rough the coming weeks will be is far more forecastable than which way prices go. A tool claiming 80 to 90% accuracy on direction has learned its past by heart. So MP forecasts a distribution, never a single number, and abstains when the evidence is thin.

Can

  • How rough the coming weeks and months will be, and the chance of a deep fall: risk is far more forecastable than direction.
  • The market's mood: calm and rising, choppy, stressed or falling.
  • What usually followed days like today, and how a holding moved after past events of a kind, with the number of cases shown.
  • A modest, honest edge on direction over months, not days, if the evidence earns it.

Cannot

  • Tomorrow's up or down: short-term moves are mostly noise, so the next-day range is wide and often abstains.
  • Rare shocks: wars and pandemics have too few past cases for confident numbers.
  • Candles or opening prices for a fund: a NAV is struck once a day (DATA-05).
  • Anything faster than the owner can act on. The 30-minute checks watch risk; they never issue a forecast.

Each bar is one day's move. The band is twice the last three weeks' volatility. The size of the moves comes in spells, so next month's roughness can be estimated; the sign of tomorrow's bar is close to a coin toss.

The rules baseline, R0

Fixed rules a person can check by hand

OWNER-SELECTED · ARC-05, baselines firstPROPOSED · PH-04 · L4-S02PROPOSED · CAP-02, CAP-03, adapted from MA

The Market Agent's rules engine becomes MP's first rung, R0: deterministic holder rules a person can check by hand, and the baseline every model has to beat on the same dates. It runs inside the daily durable workflow after each eligible close, from inputs frozen at the cutoff, and writes a rule stance for each listing to DynamoDB. Its strength score is a labelled heuristic, never a probability. The indicators, thresholds and cascade below are design inputs carried over from MA, to be specified and approved in L4-S02.

  5. The holder's cascade

Rule strength, a labelled heuristic

strength = clamp(0.5 + agreement + persistence + quality + history, 0, 0.95)

  • Agreement: +0.05 for each pillar pointing the same way, −0.05 for each against.
  • Persistence: bars the state has held, 0 for 1 bar up to +0.10 for 10 or more.
  • Quality: −0.10 for partial components, stale or provisional data, or a proxy gap over 1%.
  • History: the hit rate of past calls in the same state, once 20 or more mature outcomes exist, pulling the score toward it.

The formula, the 20-outcome history term and the 55% "no clear view" line are design inputs carried from MA, PROPOSED.

The learning loop, daily

Grade every day, correct within bands, and only the owner promotes

OWNER-SELECTED · bounded self-correction, 3 Nov 2026OWNER-SELECTED · owner promotesOWNER-SELECTED · limits, 3 Nov 2026OPEN · probation lengthPROPOSED · SCI-07PH-04 · L4-S07, L4-S08

Learning here is bounded (owner, 3 November 2026). Every forecast is frozen as it was made, with its cutoff and the versions of its task, data, features, model and calibrator, and graded only after its horizon has passed and its outcome is published. After each close, code, never a Claude agent, grades every forecast that has matured and makes small calibration and weighting adjustments by itself, inside bands the owner approves in advance; an adjustment that makes the scores worse is rolled back on its own. New models, new features, new rules and anything outside a band still wait for the owner, and the automatic safety pause (POLICY-003) stays. The limits are moderate (owner, 3 November 2026): a forecast's probabilities may shift at most 5 percentage points a week, and how much a signal counts may change at most 10% a month. Anything larger waits for the owner, and the change rolls back automatically if scores worsen. Research agents may summarise and cite sources; they cannot change model weights, publish a forecast, read secrets or trade.

  1. Freeze it as made

    Every rules call and forecast, with its cutoff and versions. Append-only: never rewritten.

    DynamoDBS3

  2. Grade after each close

    Every forecast whose horizon has passed and whose outcome is published: realised return, hit or miss, Brier, log loss, pinball loss, inside its range or not. A late correction adds a new Outcome and Score; both survive.

    DynamoDB

  3. Adjust within bands

    Code refits the calibration map and the weights that combine the views on a rolling window. A change is used only if it does better on the held-back part, only where a bucket has enough cases, and only inside its band.

    Lambda

  4. Probation

    Each adjustment is watched for a set number of trading days. One that does worse is rolled back on its own to the last good map.

  5. Report

    A line in Activity each day, the scoreboard in the weekly review, and a monthly note on what changed and why.

  6. Outside a band: the owner

    A new model, feature or rule, or any change beyond its band, becomes a request with its evidence. Only the owner approves.

Daily grading, in-band adjustment, automatic rollback and the limits (at most 5 percentage points a week for a forecast's probabilities, at most 10% a month for how much a signal counts) are OWNER-SELECTED. The probation length is OPEN; MA's 10 trading days is the starting proposal.

Self-healing on the market pipeline

Nothing lost, safe work retried, the rest raised to the owner

MP-23MP-27MP-28CT-05CT-14PL-05OWNER-SELECTEDPROPOSED details

A bad minute at Yahoo Finance, Twelve Data or the Anthropic API should cost a delay, never a day's record. The daily run is a Python workflow on Lambda Durable Functions (ARC-03, owner-selected), timed by EventBridge Scheduler, its jobs buffered on SQS with dead-letter queues. MP does not use Step Functions. The retry counts, backoff and stuck-run thresholds below are proposed, and nothing here is built.

  1. Collect

    At the close +15 minutes: prices, actions and FX, each stamped with when it became known.

  2. Retry

    At +40, one retry for a missing close, inside the day's existing credit allocation.

  3. Freeze

    At +60 the cutoff freezes. A late answer can never move it.

  4. Forecast

    By +75, approved artefacts only; otherwise waiting_data or abstained.

  5. Grade and calibrate

    Only forecasts whose horizon has passed, against a published outcome; then calibration and weights adjusted inside their bands, on probation.

  6. 07:30 brief

    Reads the stored latest origins; anything late is labelled stale. It never waits on a new batch.

Each step is a durable checkpoint: a replay resumes from the last one instead of starting again. Calls to outside services sit in their own controlled steps, so an ambiguous paid call or a started training job is reconciled, never blindly repeated. The clocks are POLICY-002 (owner-selected, still to be proven against real sources).
