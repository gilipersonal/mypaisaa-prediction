Prism

Option B for the MyPrediction design system. Every market segment is a luminous colour field, the way Monzo gives each pot its own colour. Apple's frosted glass floats over those fields for bars, tab bars and sheets. Every figure sits on a solid surface, so colour and blur never get between you and a number.

Foundations

One small set of semantic tokens drives the web app and the Apple app. Contrast below is measured in this page, live, against the background each colour actually sits on, in both themes.

Colour: surfaces, ink and meaning

Neutrals lean slightly towards cobalt so the glass and the fields feel related. Colour carries meaning only with a second signal: an arrow and a sign for change, an icon and a word for status.

Colour: the segment fields

Hue family follows the market: blues and violets for the US, magentas and copper for India, lagoon for the UK, pewter for metals. Each field carries white text at 4.5:1 or better, in both themes. A field never means gain or loss.

Chart marks for the market split use their own validated steps (--mark-us #2F54EB, --mark-uk #00A0A8, --mark-in #B5287E), which pass all six palette checks on both chart surfaces, colour-blind separation included. Every chart also names its series in a legend.

Type

Bricolage Grotesque gives titles and amounts a confident, slightly inky voice; Instrument Sans keeps lists and figures calm and compact. Both carry true tabular figures, switched on everywhere numbers appear. On Apple, each style scales with Dynamic Type through relativeTo:, with SF Pro as the fallback.

Icons

One drawn set on a 24-point grid, 1.75 stroke, round ends; SF Symbols take their place in the Apple app. Icons beside a word are hidden from screen readers; icon-only controls carry a label.

Each module is composed the same way on every screen. States are drawn, not described. These follow the theme toggle at the top of the page.

Segment tiles and the top-5 card

Tiles are the colourful entry points; the list inside is always on a solid surface. A score appears only when growth, protection and opportunity are all eligible. A missing fifth place is said, not hidden.

Forecast range and price chart

A forecast is never a bare number. History is a solid line; the live forecast is a saffron fan (50% inner, 80% outer) around a median; an evaluated past forecast is an outline with its mint outcome; scenarios are dotted and claim no likelihood. Hover or use the arrow keys on the chart for exact values.

Ranked-action badge

Decision support, with a reason and the uncertainty beside it. The icon carries the meaning; the tint is a hint, not the message.

NVIDIA, 6 months

NVIDIA is 13.5% of the portfolio and has the widest 80% range of your holdings (−18.5% to +34.0%). Trimming would narrow the portfolio's range. Decision support only: MP never places trades.

Approval sheet and toast
