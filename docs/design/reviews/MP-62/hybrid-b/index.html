Prism

Every market segment owns a field of colour, the way Monzo gives each pot its own. The fields are deep, so white words on them stay sharp at 20% screen brightness, and each glows in its corner. Everything else is MyPaisa's warm ledger: paper, white cards, a navy hero card, rounded figures and iOS springs. Glass carries only the iPhone and iPad controls. Colour is the field; ink is the word.

Hybrid A · Fan shares this UX system: the same type, shapes, components, states, patterns and motion. Only the identity differs.

01

Principles

Six rules decide every screen. Each one says what it rules out, so a builder can tell when a design breaks it.

02

Foundations

MyPaisa's type, shapes, surfaces and motion, on one token sheet for the web and the Apple apps. Every value below is read live from this page's CSS, both themes at once.

Colour: ground, surfaces and ink

Warm paper, white cards and near-black ink in light; near-black ground and white ink in dark. At 20% brightness the best any pair can do is 5:1, so primary text sits at the extremes and the warmth lives in the paper, not in grey text.

Colour: meaning, action and forecast

One action colour, deep indigo in light and moonlight in dark, because a filled button's label must reach 4.5:1 at 20%. Forecasts are violet light: a median line over two bands. Scored outcomes are lagoon teal, scenarios plum and dotted. Gains are green with ▲; a routine fall is quiet ink with ▼; crimson is kept for alerts that ask for action.

The segment fields

Eight fields, one per segment, the same in both themes so a segment is recognised anywhere. US segments are blues and violet, India's are saffron, orchid and fuchsia, the UK's is lagoon, metals are gold. Each field is a deep base (white labels hold the body tier, 4.5:1 at 20%) lit by its glow, which also draws the field's edge, the hero's prism bar and the instrument coins. A field never means gain or loss.

Type

Figures in SF Pro Rounded (Nunito on the web) at 800, tabular, tracked −0.03em. Everything else in SF Pro (Inter on the web) on Apple's Dynamic Type Large scale. Model ids and hashes in SF Mono. Apple devices use the system faces and download nothing.

Figures

Big amounts set the pence small. Columns line up digit for digit, signs use a true minus, and a figure that rolls keeps its width, so nothing beside it moves.

Materials: glass on the controls, never on content

Liquid Glass carries the iPhone tab dock and its bottom accessory, the iPad sidebar, toolbars and sheets. Content (the hero, cards, lists, charts, tiles) is never glass, glass never sits on glass, and the web PWA uses solid paper bars instead. At 20% brightness glass has to be dense, so its fill is 97% (light) and 94% (dark); the blur still shows colour moving beneath. Reduce Transparency makes it opaque.

Icons

Material Symbols Rounded on the web, SF Symbols in the Apple apps; the mapping names both. An icon beside a word is hidden from screen readers; an icon alone carries a label.

03

Components

Each module is composed the same way everywhere, and its states are drawn, not described. They follow the theme in the bar above.

Buttons

No buy, sell or order control exists anywhere. The strongest action in MP is recording a decision in your own journal. 50 pt primary, 44 pt small; a press scales to 0.97 in the same frame.

Segmented control and the horizon switch

The selected segment is a strong fill (white words on indigo in light, indigo words on moonlight in dark) so it is plain at 20%; its lens slides by transform. The 3, 6 and 12-month horizons lead; 1 to 20 sessions sit apart, labelled short-term.

Short-term forecasts carry their own amber label and their own calibration, and are never shown as investing guidance. On iPhone the horizon switch lives in the bottom accessory, in thumb reach.

Chips: status, freshness and provider

Every chip pairs a mark or an icon with a word, so it survives grayscale, colour blindness and 20% brightness.

The hero card

MyPaisa's navy hero, one per screen. On the portfolio it carries the prism bar: your money split by segment, each piece in its field's glow. On an instrument it takes that instrument's segment field and carries the forecast fan.

Holding row

Coin, name, venue, value and today's change on the first line; the 6-month range, its median and the ranked action on the second. Every range in a list shares one scale (−20% to +36%), so rows compare at a glance. The coin wears its segment's field.

Segment tiles and the top-5 card

Tiles are the colourful way in; the list inside is always on a solid card. A score shows only when growth, protection and opportunity are all eligible, and a missing fifth place is said, not hidden.

Default · selected · forecast unavailable

Forecast card and chart

A forecast is never a bare number. History is a slate line; the live forecast is a violet median over its 50% and 80% bands; an evaluated past forecast is a dashed outline with its teal outcome. Scrub the chart with a pointer or the arrow keys: a lollipop reads out each month.

Ranked action

Add, hold, pause, trim and rebalance are decision support: each comes with its reason and its uncertainty, in neutral ink, never as an instruction.

Sheet and toast

Every confirmation is a sheet. It records a decision in your journal and never moves money; its figures sit on a solid card inside the glass.

Navigation: tab dock, sidebar, web bars, command bar

iPhone: a floating glass tab dock with a search orb, and a bottom accessory for the horizon. iPad: a floating glass sidebar. Web: solid paper, a 248 pt sidebar from 1024 px with a ⌘K command bar, a bottom tab bar below. The selected item is the same strong lens everywhere.

04

Motion

MyPaisa's motion (MA-182), held to a smoothness budget: quick, settled iOS springs that move only transform and opacity. Every demo replays; each names its SwiftUI form.

Curves and durations

Smooth for content arriving, snappy (a small overshoot) for a control answering a touch, bouncy only for a success, the system curve for sheets. Fast 180, base 340, slow 520, draw 950, count 750 ms; siblings 45 ms apart, at most eight.

Arrivals, the chart, the figure and the lens

Each frame plays once as it scrolls into view, started by an IntersectionObserver, never a scroll handler.

    05

    The UX system

    One system for both hybrids: how a forecast is read, how navigation changes with width, the patterns, every state, and where the thumb reaches.

    Reading a forecast in three depths

    The same three depths on every surface: a row, a card, a sheet, a widget. Each depth is one tap from the next, and none is ever skipped.

    Navigation by width, not device

    The layout reads its own width. An iPhone is always compact; an iPad in Split View can be compact or regular; a laptop window can be any of the three.

    Patterns

    Six patterns recur on every screen. Each is drawn once here and used the same way everywhere.

    States, and each at 20%

    Freshness and the provider stay on screen in every state, the skeleton included. Under each state is the same part through the 20% filter, so you can see it hold.

    One-handed reach

    On iPhone the horizon switch and the primary actions sit in the lower half: the horizon in the bottom accessory above the tab dock, a decision's Record button at the foot of its sheet. Search and the profile, used less, may sit at the top.

    06

    Screens

    Five screens at true size: nothing is scaled, so every word is as large as on the device. They follow the theme in the bar: switch to Dark to see each one at night. The wide boards scroll sideways inside their frame on a narrow window, or fit to its width.

    iPhone · home

    Holdings first, under the hero. Scroll on, and the segment fields follow as a carousel with the top 5 of the selected one. The horizon switch rides in the bottom accessory, above the glass tab dock.

    iPhone · instrument detail

    Apple at 6 months. The hero takes US tech's field and carries the fan; the full chart, the proof and the ranked action follow, each one tap from the next.

    iPad · split view

    Glass sidebar, the holdings list and the selected instrument's forecast side by side, at regular-to-wide width.

    Desktop · web dashboard

    Solid paper chrome, a 248 pt sidebar, the ⌘K command bar and 1.3 : 1 columns: holdings with shared-scale ranges, the selected forecast, its proof, the fields and provider health.

    PWA · 390 px

    The web app at phone width: a solid top bar, the bottom tab bar, denser rows and keyboard focus drawn.

Body 7 and 4.5, text 4.5 and 3, marks 3 and 1.8, presence 1.5 and 1.25. A ground written a + b is a over b. Turn on Preview at 20% in the bar to see the model on your own screen.
