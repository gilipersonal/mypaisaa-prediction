MyPrediction design systemOption A: Fan

Show the range, score every forecast. The MP mark becomes the system: a price line climbs to now, opens into a saffron fan of 50% and 80% ranges, and a mint dot lands where the outcome fell. Monzo's bold, rounded modules sit on Apple's structure, on warm paper by day and deep ink by night.

Proposed Recommended option MP-62 · options round 1 Not approved UX · no build All figures illustrative

Why recommended: the identity and the product's promise are the same thing. Every forecast carries its range, and every scored forecast shows where it landed. It gives up some of Option B's spectacle for a look nobody else can have.

Foundations

Semantic tokens with a light value and a dark value each. Contrast is measured live in this page against the background each colour actually sits on, in both themes.

Colour

Ink navy and warm paper carry the brand. Saffron means forecast, and only forecast. Mint means a scored outcome, and only that. Up and down always come with a sign and a triangle, so colour is never the only signal. Status colours are kept apart from both.

Type

Gabarito (display, 700 to 900) gives amounts and titles their round, confident weight. Hanken Grotesk (text, 400 to 700) carries reading and data, with tabular figures for every column of numbers. Each style maps to a SwiftUI text style so it scales with Dynamic Type; web sizes follow the same steps in px.

Numerals

Amounts are big pounds with smaller pence. Columns use tabular figures so digits line up. Negative values use a true minus sign. Changes always carry their sign and a triangle.

Spacing, radii and elevation

A 4-point grid. Radii come from the mark's tile (28 on 120): modules use 24 to 28, so cards feel soft and friendly without turning into pills. Elevation is a soft offset shadow on paper; on ink it adds a lighter surface step.

Materials

Translucency belongs to the control layer only: tab bars, navigation bars, the page top bar, sheets. It is never placed under a financial figure, because blur behind a number makes it harder to read and suggests it is less certain than it is.

Icons

Drawn on a 24-point grid with a 1.75 stroke, round caps and round joins, to match SF Symbols' regular weight beside Hanken Grotesk. The forecast icon is the fan itself. Icons beside a label are hidden from screen readers; icon-only controls carry a name.

Motion

One signature moment: the fan unfolds from the now point the first time a forecast appears, and the mint outcome lands with a single small spring. Everything else is quick and quiet. With Reduce Motion on, every motion becomes its final frame or a 120 ms cross-fade.

Components

Each component is shown in every state it can be in. Controls on this page work: try the horizon selector, the segmented control and the chart hover.

Buttons

One primary action per screen. MP has no buy, sell or order control anywhere; the strongest action is recording your own decision.

Segmented control and horizon selector

The horizon selector is a fan of steps: each long-term horizon is taller than the last, so 12 months reads as the widest view. Short-term horizons trail behind it, smaller, outlined and labelled short-term.

Chips

Status labels for decisions, freshness and provider status, and the four kinds of figure. Each chip pairs its colour with a word and, where it helps, an icon.

Holding row

Monzo's plain list row with a round logo, carrying a forecast in a small fan glyph: now on the left, the 6-month 50% and 80% ranges opening to the right, on one shared scale so rows compare.

Top 5 card

A rounded module per segment. The score shows only when growth, protection and opportunity are all eligible; an empty rank says why.

Forecast range

The range in three densities: full with labelled values, the ribbon for tables, and the fan glyph for rows. A forecast never appears as a bare number.

Price chart with forecast and scored outcome

Historical prices are a solid ink line. The live forecast is a filled saffron fan. A past forecast is an open dashed fan with its mint outcome where the price landed. Scenarios are dotted and claim no likelihood. Hover or touch the chart for values; a table version sits beneath.

Ranked-action badge

Decision support, not advice. Each action has its own shape, so the five read apart without colour. Pause is the only tinted one, because it says the data is not fit to rank.

Approval sheet and toast

The sheet records the owner's own decision in the journal. It states plainly that nothing is traded.

Navigation: tab bar, sidebar and search

A tab bar on iPhone and the PWA at phone width; a sidebar from 768 px. Layout follows width, not device name.

States

Every state is designed. Freshness and provider status stay visible in all of them, including while loading.

Screens

Each screen in light and dark. Phone screens scroll inside the device. The figures are the shared illustrative set for MP-62.

Mapping

Each token and component is named once. The web PWA builds from the CSS custom property; the Apple app builds from the SwiftUI name. Colours live in the asset catalogue with Any and Dark appearances.
