    The rules, in plain words

    What every builder keeps to, and what the gates measure.

      Claude

      Numbers come from code and models. Words come from Claude. The motion says which is which: the mark moves only while Claude works, every figure it writes carries its source, and nothing moves once the decision is yours.

      Four beats

      • Reading

        Your question lands. The mark's three facets light in turn, and each read-only step lands as its tick draws.

      • Writing

        Sentences fade in one at a time. Figures are bold and each carries its source number.

      • Citing

        The source chips land in turn. A figure with no source never appears.

      • Handing back

        The actions rise and the mark rests. Claude proposes, you decide, and nothing moves again.

      Four states that stand still

      • Stopped

        You stopped it. Nothing changed and no answer was saved.

      • Refused

        MyPrediction cannot trade, so Claude says so and offers the forecast instead.

      • No evidence

        No model or source covers the question, so Claude will not guess.

      • Claude off

        Numbers never depend on Claude: forecasts, ranges and scores all still work.

      Contrast at 20%

      Everything here has to read on a phone turned down to a fifth of its light. Every pair below is measured from this page's own tokens when it loads, so the table cannot drift from what is drawn.

      The model

      Turn a screen down and its own light falls to a fifth, but the room's reflection stays. So pale things fall a long way and dark things hardly move, and the gap between them shrinks. For two colours of relative luminance L1 and L2, with L1 the lighter: at full brightness the ratio is (L1 + 0.05) / (L2 + 0.05), WCAG 2.2's formula. At 20% it is (0.2 L1 + 0.05) / (0.2 L2 + 0.05). A ground written a + b is a composited over b.

      The owner chose MA's ink. Two inks, #14171F and #4E5462 in light, #F3F5F8 and #C5CCD7 in dark. So the text tier is the 20% rule as first set, 4.5:1 and 3:1, not round 3's raised one. To let that ink hold on Prism's grounds, the light ground rises a touch, the dark card deepens a touch, and glass is denser at 72%.

      In the Apple app

      What the platform does for us, and how Prism uses it.

      Dynamic Type on every style

      Every text style maps to a system style and scales with the reader's setting. Figures use .system(size:, weight: .heavy, design: .rounded) with relativeTo:. At Accessibility sizes a row stacks and nothing truncates a figure.

      SF Symbols

      Symbols carry the facet with .symbolRenderingMode(.hierarchical). A selected tab uses .palette with the link colour. Line-only symbols stay .monochrome.

      Glass on the control layer

      .glassEffect() carries the tab bar, the sidebar, toolbars and sheets. Never on content, never on a figure, never glass on glass. The web keeps backdrop-filter at 24 px or less.

      Size classes

      Compact width takes TabView; regular width takes NavigationSplitView. Widths match the web's classes, and windows on iPad change class as they resize.

      Haptics

      .sensoryFeedback(.selection, trigger:) on a horizon change and a chart scrub. .success when a decision is recorded. Nothing else buzzes.

      Reduce Motion and Transparency

      Read accessibilityReduceMotion: arrivals land at once and a change of state is a 150 ms cross-fade. Read accessibilityReduceTransparency: glass turns solid.

      Prism changes in the open, on a known path.

      Versions

      • MajorA token or component is removed or renamed, or a meaning changes.
      • MinorSomething is added and nothing that exists changes.
      • PatchA value moves and every pair still passes.

      Proposing a change

      1. File itA Jira issue on the MP board, with the reason.
      2. Draw itChange tokens.json and this page on the issue's branch.
      3. Measure itThe token check and both design gates pass.
      4. Approve itThe owner approves, and the status label moves.

      Deprecation

      • Mark itThe label turns Deprecated and names the replacement.
      • Keep itIt stays for one minor version, and both platforms keep building.
      • Remove itOnly in a major version, with the change noted here.

      Status labels

      • Proposed
        Drawn, not yet chosen.
      • Owner-selected

      • Implemented

      • Deprecated
