PLANNING DRAFT MP-20 Foundation Pack 1. Architecture baseline unapproved; no UX or implementation. Maker checks only; independent review has not run. Budget status conditional/unproven.

Owner-confirmed detail: next-day plus 5/10/20 trading-day points; 3/6/12 calendar-month anniversaries roll to a preceding valid local session/valuation date. Canonical-value availability controls scoring. Exact daily tolerances and policy parameters remain proposed. The source twelve directions are in the existing MP-19 planning index.

This proposed specification addresses MP-27/MP-29, FC-01, PL-10/12/13 and CT-06/13. It uses public primary prices checked on 2 November 2026 and reproducible decimal arithmetic. model.json contains every quantity, unit, line cost and scenario; public-price-evidence.json pins regional offer-file URLs, publication dates, hashes, SKUs and rate codes. These are estimates for an unbuilt system, not a bill or a claim of deployment readiness.

COST-01 — Confirmed envelope. The owner selected a combined £100/month cash ceiling for dev AWS, prod AWS, data, Anthropic and evaluations, with £70 planned operation and £30 reserve, warning at £60, reduced optional work at £75 and paused optional work at £85. Separate workload accounts share consolidated cost visibility and this one envelope. The older £50 AWS-only target is superseded. A single shared $200 Anthropic organisation credit is owner-reported and unverified; admissible credit is currently zero. Do not duplicate AWS free tiers, provider quotas or shared credits across accounts/apps.

COST-02 — Workload and dimensions. The reference is one owner, 30 days, 20 illustrative listings, one daily origin per listing and up to 16 eligible monitoring slots/session. Actual holdings and listing eligibility are unknown. The seven formal horizons are next day, 5/10/20 trading days and 3/6/12 calendar months (FND-DEC-001/002), using the confirmed anniversary/preceding-valid-session convention and waiting for the canonical target value. This does not authorise seven independently trained families for every listing. A proposed two-campaign monthly training cap covers one declared task/horizon/market-kind scope per campaign; qualifications rotate or remain unavailable when the cap is insufficient. The cap is a proposal, not owner-approved scientific adequacy.

The baseline training total is 12 ml.m5.large instance-hours across both accounts (10 prod, 2 dev), equivalent to 24 vCPU-hours on a 2-vCPU instance. AWS bills the instance's 8 GiB even if the workload uses at most 4 GiB. Campaigns, retries, refits, calibration and evaluation share that bound. There is no free standing endpoint, notebook, GPU, managed MLflow server or provisioned concurrency. Baselines may run in the ordinary compute budget; the SageMaker allowance is for explicitly admitted candidates. Temporary training volumes are included in the ancillary allowance until exact storage configuration is known.

The baseline token allocations in model.json total $4.775 gross/month, including dev, prod and $2.00 of evaluations. Formula: sum(input_MTok × input_rate + output_MTok × output_rate) per route/environment. No cache or batch discount is assumed. Cache writes/hits have distinct rates and must be metered when used; a small repeated prompt does not automatically earn caching savings. Tool/search calls, retry outputs, batch liabilities and human/online judging require their own reservation. This specification executes none of them.

COST-06 — Conservative admission. Proposed admission uses an atomic reservation in the aggregate monthly ledger, scoped by owner/environment/route/job and idempotency key. Define encumbered_cash = settled_cash + outstanding_unreconciled_liability + future_fixed_commitments + open_reservations, avoiding overlap when a reservation settles. available = min(global_remaining, environment_remaining, route_remaining). Admit only when worst-case tax/FX-adjusted additional cash fits all limits; optional Claude also requires sufficient verified credit allocated through completion, regardless of cash headroom. A timeout/batch/retry with unknown external outcome keeps its liability. Cancellation releases only confirmed unspent liability; an AWS stop request is not proof that billing ceased.

The £70 plan must include subscription commitments at the start of the month. Proposed internal allocations, subject to owner agreement, are data ≤£30, AWS ≤£22, Claude/evals ≤£10 and forecast/price uncertainty ≤£8, across both accounts. They are planning ceilings, not independently spendable pots. A £75.84 fixed subscription fails the data/operation admission before optional jobs start. At £60 warn, at £75 reduce optional backfill/challengers/LLM work, at £85 pause optional work and preserve a proposed £15 emergency window for authentication, ledger reads, audit and recovery. This cannot guarantee that delayed cloud invoices stop exactly at £100; hard request/concurrency/timeout/retention caps and fixed commitments remain necessary. No autonomous feed upgrade or price-driven feature removal is allowed.

COST-07 — Telemetry and billing collection. Business/run/approval evidence remains durable even when traces are sampled out. Logs have content redaction, bounded dimensions, explicit retention and scan limits. The model prices metric series rather than assuming every route×listing×outcome is one metric. Trace storage/retrieval, CloudWatch logs and queries are priced separately; no paid tracking server is assumed. S3 originals, model artefacts, backups and evaluation transcripts are included in the storage quantities, subject to permitted retention and policy approval.

Cost Explorer charges per paginated request. The baseline allows 120 primary-view pages/month ($1.20): daily AWS collection, as confirmed by DEC-025 (owner choice C03), covering both accounts by grouping where practical. The 120-page figure is a conservative monthly paginated-request cap, for example 30 daily collections at no more than 4 pages each, rather than a six-hourly AWS schedule. Additional pages consume the same cap; exhausted pagination is deferred or reported partial. Anthropic usage/billing collection is separately confirmed at six-hour intervals. Its delayed usage/cost reports do not establish available credit. The app dashboard reads cached aggregates and reservation records; refreshing the dashboard never polls billing APIs. AWS documents that billing refresh is delayed and some data arrives after 24 hours. Display source time, collected time, estimate/reconciled status and unknown liabilities. Proposed AWS report age >36 hours blocks relevant optional admission until reconciled. A separate proposed maximum credit-allocation proof age of 30 minutes is an admission condition, not the six-hour Anthropic usage/billing collection interval and not an assertion that such proof is available. A six-hour usage report alone cannot satisfy that condition or prove a balance. These freshness thresholds need owner approval; absent trustworthy allocation/freshness proof, optional Claude dispatch remains refused and admissible credit remains zero.

COST-08/09 — Stress, decisions and proof. The uncontrolled stress scenario increases general calls 5×, monitor-related data/log/trace and billing activity 10×, uses 6,000 prod/1,000 dev SSE sessions of 600 seconds, 56 total training instance-hours, 60 custom metrics, more builds/storage and $91.75 model/eval gross. It deliberately violates the proposed training/admission caps. It proves the importance of bounded admission, not that the application is allowed to spend £274. One continuously held 0.5GB stream for 30 days alone costs $21.60 compute before requests/network at these rates; reconnects and multiple devices add liability. Price a continuous-connection design separately rather than treating SSE as free transport.
