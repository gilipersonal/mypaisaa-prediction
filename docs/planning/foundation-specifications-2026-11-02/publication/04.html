SCI-01 · Task identity, clocks and horizon maturity

A ForecastTask is an immutable, versioned definition, not a loose phrase such as “four-week confidence”. Its key binds target equation, horizon unit/value, instrument capability, return currency, observation grid, origin rule, maturity roll, distribution support, event threshold, benchmark mapping, data/adjustment/calendar/FX policies and metrics. Changing any of these creates a new task version. A prediction cannot be relabelled with a more favourable target afterwards.

The bounded catalogue retains owner-confirmed next day and 5/10/20 trading days, plus 3/6/12 months (FND-DEC-001). The proposed next-day implementation is the next eligible local valuation session; calendar-month anniversaries with preceding valid local valuation dates are now owner-confirmed (FND-DEC-002). Exact origin/freshness tolerances remain proposed. Calendar months are not 63/126/252 sessions. Those approximations may be descriptive metadata only. Retain every agreed horizon even if evidence or source rights prevent numerical issuance. Adding every integer from5through20 would be a new scope/capacity decision; it is not part of the confirmed5/10/20catalogue.

For a listed security, origin_session is the local exchange session whose final close is used. origin_at is its actual closing timestamp with IANA zone and UTC timestamp, including half-days and daylight-saving changes. data_cutoff is the latest permissible known_at for inputs, fixed before prediction creation. issued_at must be at or after cutoff and before the next eligible session opens; a later job records missed_origin and does not backdate a forecast. A proposed 60-minute finalisation grace after close is a deadline policy, not settlement of the security transaction, and requires per-source evidence. An observation arriving after cutoff cannot be retroactively admitted. Historical replay has a separate recorded replay_cutoff and availability-quality classification; today’s ingestion does not prove historical availability.

Session maturity counts strictly after the origin on that listing's frozen calendar. Calendar-month maturity follows owner-confirmed anniversaries and preceding eligible local valuation session when closed (FND-DEC-002). The explicit invalid-date engineering convention is to preserve day-of-month where possible and clamp to the last valid day, then apply that preceding-session roll. Never recalculate an old prediction's maturity against a newly downloaded calendar silently: calendar corrections create an amendment with the original retained. Publication lag affects when the label is available, not which economic date it measures. A NAV effective on Friday but published Monday is Friday's valuation; Monday ingestion is not Monday performance. NAV schedules use administrator valuation calendars, not assumed exchange candles. Missing scheduled NAV causes a gap; a proxy is a separately named estimate and cannot become ground truth.

Labels mature only after all required terminal, path, action, FX and benchmark observations are available under the outcome policy. The economic maturity date can precede outcome_known_at. Pending, missing_path, missing_fx, disputed_action, suspended and delisted_unresolved differ from a zero return. Thirty-minute eligible quote/risk monitors produce monitoring records with their own timestamps; they never advance the daily forecast's origin or rerun all horizons implicitly.

Official exchange calendars have exceptions, including half-days; NYSE's published calendar explicitly includes early closes and LSE publishes its business-day rules. A calendar is versioned input, not “Monday to Friday”. NSE publishes market timings and holiday schedules separately. The specific session arrays in the golden cases are artificial miniature calendars, except the clearly identified official holiday example. NYSE, LSE, NSE.

SCI-02 · Price, distributions and ground truth

Maintain three separate series identities: raw quoted OHLC/NAV with unit, split/corporate-action-normalised price features, and a self-financing total-return index (TRI) for economic return labels. GBX is divided by 100 exactly once before GBP arithmetic. A two-for-one split changes units and price, not wealth. Total return reinvests the specified distribution at the declared ex-date valuation under a gross-before-personal-tax research convention. Taxes, fees and ledger cash dividends are separate economic records; never add dividends again to a TRI. Net-of-withholding or fee variants require separate task identities. Missing action treatment blocks a claimed total-return outcome.

Let I_j be a nonnegative local-currency TRI with strictly positive I_0, q_j GBP per one local currency, and W_j=I_j q_j on the task's declared observation grid j=0…H. Local-only tasks use W_j=I_j. Native and GBP variants are different tasks; a local forecast cannot silently claim GBP evaluation. The proposed owner reporting base is GBP. FX pairing uses the latest eligible observation at or before each valuation timestamp within a separately approved tolerance; no future FX quote or silent unlimited carry-forward. Report actual FX timestamps. Economic exposure currency is a different attribute from listing currency.

The target equations are:

  • Terminal total return R=W_H/W_0−1; a return distribution is the predictive CDF/quantiles of R, not a single direction score. q10/q50/q90 are the proposed compact range, representing an 80% central interval; they are not guaranteed bounds.
  • Up event U=1[R>0], equality is not up. P(up) is an event probability. Rule strength, extraction confidence and preference scores are different fields.
  • Benchmark excess E=R−R_b, where benchmark b has the same task currency, exact economic endpoints and compatible total-return convention. B=1[E>0]. Arithmetic difference is explicitly chosen; relative wealth ratio is another possible target and cannot substitute without a new version. No approved benchmark mapping means benchmark probability is unavailable, even when R is available.
  • Peak-to-trough maximum drawdown D=max_j(1−W_j/max_{0≤k≤j}W_k). The origin belongs to the path. Close-sampled D must be called “maximum drawdown on the daily valuation path”, not intraday maximum loss.
  • Origin-relative adverse excursion A=max(0,1−min_j W_j/W_0). A and D differ when a new peak occurs after origin.
  • Terminal loss event L_tau=1[R≤−tau]. Within-horizon risk events 1[D≥delta] and 1[A≥alpha] are different tasks. Thresholds and inclusive boundaries are task-versioned owner-pending choices.
  • Cost-basis loss compares owner ledger cost and valuation, with flows/lots/FX rules from the financial lane. It is a historical/accounting measure, not D, A or a label intrinsic to an instrument return forecast.

A terminal distribution alone does not identify a path-risk distribution. No monotonic relation between the event probabilities is assumed except logically required relationships under the same complete path (for example D≥A). Within-horizon risk uses a separately fitted/calibrated event estimator or a validated joint-path distribution. If unavailable, return quantiles may be issued while path-risk probability abstains. Proposed 5% short-horizon and 10% longer-horizon examples are illustrative event definitions, not selected protection thresholds.

The root shared conventions apply: decimal strings, GBP-per-local FX, separate economic/known/ingestion times and separate entitlement/data-quality/metric-eligibility states. Record execution mode (synthetic, permitted recording or live) differs from observation mode (synthetic, observed, derived or scenario). Prediction, Outcome, Score, Eligibility and PromotionRecord are append-only. Prediction freezes numerical outputs before outcomes, plus model/calibrator/features/manifest/task revisions and hashes. Outcome records canonical observations, first-available or amended status, event values and evidence links. The canonical source/type and revision/dispute selection policy is frozen before outcomes; source switching to improve scores is forbidden. A corrected canonical close or action creates a new Outcome linked by supersedes; both original and corrected scores survive with policy-labelled aggregates. Score identifies exactly which outcome revision it used. No erasure of poor forecasts or reinterpretation after delisting. Bankruptcy proceeds can be zero only with evidence; missing delisting price is unresolved, not zero and not removal from the universe.

SCI-03 · Dataset and feature admission

Every observation carries event/effective time, publication time if evidenced, ingested_at, conservative known_at, revision lineage, source and content hash. For prospective decisions known_at=max(evidenced publication, ingestion). Historical replay may use independently documented historical availability, recorded as a different provenance field and replay-quality policy; a guessed publication date is not point-in-time proof. Revisions arriving today cannot enter yesterday's feature set. ALFRED/FRED supports vintage parameters, but that capability does not make every downloaded series vintage-correct automatically. FRED observation parameters.

The manifest freezes listing identities, contemporaneous universe membership, delisted/merged histories, exposure/constituent snapshots, raw inputs, corporate actions, FX, calendars, feature code/schema and rights evidence. Current constituents must not define the historical research universe without survivorship qualification. A fixed owner watchlist can support a declared current-watchlist study, but cannot claim unbiased universe-wide generalisation. Cross-listed securities require an economic-entity identifier for dependence analysis and leakage grouping; separate listings are not independent samples.

Initial feature blocks are lagged total/price returns, trailing volatility/peak risk, volume only where reliable, declared market context, optional vintage-backed fundamentals/macro, and missingness flags. All windows end at cutoff. Scaling, imputation, winsorisation, feature selection, dimensionality reduction, regime thresholds, analog distance normalisation and calibration are fit only inside the allowed training/calibration partitions. No complete-dataset scaler followed by temporal splitting. Missingness is not proof of zero exposure. NAV-only observations prohibit OHLC candle features and intraday promises.

Data status is vintage_backed, lag_conservative, prospective_only or unavailable, separately for each block and listing. A model may issue only with the exact approved feature contract; dropping news at runtime requires a separately qualified missing-news variant. A training or Anthropic-sharing licence is independently required; no source activation is authorised here.

SCI-04 · Model and module catalogue

R0 comprises deterministic holder rules, uncalibrated heuristic strength, zero-return/no-change baseline, trailing historical event-rate baseline and historical empirical/block-return distribution. Historical windows are proposed as 252 and 756 eligible observations where available; both count as tried configurations. Constant base-rate probabilities are proper-score comparators. Rules are evaluated on the same eligible dates; only an independently qualified probability mapping may turn rule outputs into event probabilities. A historical empirical forecast is not automatically calibrated, especially under regime change.

R1 is the regularised CPU family: ridge/elastic-net-style terminal-return regression and regularised logistic classifiers for separately defined binary events. A residual distribution is proposed for ranges only if its out-of-sample distribution/coverage is qualified. R2 is a bounded boosted-tree family, with separate mean, quantile and event estimator artefacts as needed. HistGradientBoosting's documented quantile loss makes it a candidate implementation, not a selected package/version or evidence of finance performance. Quantile crossing is rejected or corrected by an approved monotone mapping trained without test access, with correction recorded. Ridge, HistGradientBoostingRegressor.

Two trainable families do not mean two trained objects. An artefact key includes task/horizon/currency/market-kind pool/features/fold/training cutoff/calibrator/schema. A pooled model may share evidence across instruments only under a declared identity and weighting policy, leaving each market/kind eligibility explicit. There is no default one-model-per-ticker explosion. Unsupported tasks remain visible as requirements with baseline-only or unavailable state.

F3 regime and risk is an explicit module combining deterministic trailing summaries and, only if qualified, event estimates. F4 analog/event research uses predeclared lagged features and standardised distance, proposes at most 20 neighbours, and admits only historical cases whose full target interval and outcome publication precede the current origin. Overlapping windows are grouped and effective evidence shown. The proposed F3 descriptive regime uses the sign of trailing60-session return and trailing20 log-return volatility above a fit-only80th-percentile threshold; missing windows produce no regime. F4 analog features are trailing20/60 returns,20-session volatility and60-session daily-path MDD, proposed756-session lookback, equal neighbour weights and deterministic distance/date/identity tie breaks. Greedy nearest-first selection removes overlapping label windows within one economic entity, while retaining cross-asset dependence disclosures. The first proposed event module is scheduled equity earnings: last completed valuation before an evidenced release, then separate5/20-session return/excess outcomes. Public release timestamps, confounding and comparator cohorts are frozen before outcomes; after-close news cannot enter the prior close predictor. Event studies predeclare event availability time, window, confounding and comparator; association is not causation. No eight-event or 30-neighbour magic threshold.

F5 direction/range is the versioned task-output service. F8 patterns proposes two bounded causal detectors: trailing 20/60-session moving-average crossing and trailing 20-session range breakout, confirmed using only completed bars. Parameter alternatives consume trial budget. Candle extensions remain retained requirements but are not activated on NAV. Detector correctness and incremental predictive value are distinct checks. F1 is the provenance lake; F2 is the temporal qualification lab; F6 is immutable proof; F7 is contribution experimentation with matched cash-flow comparators and financial-lane accounting. All F1–F8 remain retained.

LLM factors are typed, tool-free extraction with source/entity/relevance labels and model/prompt/schema/extraction timestamps. Claude runs on Anthropic behind separate route workers and a shared typed client library; it neither supplies price truth nor approves models. Inspect AI evaluates extraction/agent scenarios, evidence fidelity and policy behaviour. Those scores never replace Brier, log loss, return loss or path-risk validation. Historical extraction by a newer LLM may reflect future knowledge; prompt instructions to ignore the future are insufficient evidence. Such factors default to prospective-only unless a bounded contamination study supports a narrower claim.

SCI-05 · Predeclared qualification experiment

Chronological expanding-origin evaluation is the default proposal; rolling five-year fit windows are one challenger choice consuming search capacity. Split boundaries use common UTC date blocks across assets, retaining local origins within blocks. Assets on the same market day remain together; random ticker-row cross-validation is forbidden. Outer validation origin blocks propose four consecutive 3-month blocks for session tasks, and four 12-month blocks for month tasks. Minimum estimator-fit histories propose 36 months short and 60 months long. Chronological calibration blocks propose the latest 6 months short and 24 months long before each validation block, subject to purging. The final test proposes the last 24 months of fully mature origins, quarantined before exploration. These are resource/evidence proposals (SCI-D03), not a claim all ten-year datasets can support long-horizon qualification.

For any boundary b, exclude from an earlier fit/calibration partition any label with outcome_known_at≥b or label interval intersecting the next partition's protected origin/outcome region. Training features must independently obey their historical cutoff. Use complete label intervals, not a hardcoded 20-row gap. Proposed pre-boundary separation equals the exact task horizon plus one eligible valuation session as a conservative availability embargo; publication delay can lengthen it. A 12-month task therefore needs calendar-aware separation and sufficient older observations. In forward-only evaluation there are no later training rows in the fold; if a later reuse design allows them, purge intersecting labels and embargo post-validation origins for at least the task horizon. This is a specified policy beyond the generic gap parameter in TimeSeriesSplit.

Hyperparameters are selected inside the earlier fit region with chronological inner splits, never by outer-validation or final-test outcomes. Calibrator selection also belongs inside earlier data; a fixed proposed sigmoid map is fit on the separate chronological calibration block after estimator selection. If this leaves too little evidence, output uncalibrated/abstain; do not recover sample size by leaking future labels. Isotonic and conformal calibration are retained candidate methods requiring a separately budgeted protocol. Calibration maps freeze with each promoted artefact; daily scoring does not mutate them. The official calibration guide requires separation of estimator and calibration data, and explains that proper scores combine calibration, discrimination and outcome uncertainty. scikit-learn calibration.

The final test receives one frozen candidate-versus-baselines submission per declared campaign. In the92-fit illustration the final four fits represent one frozen R1 learned baseline and one preselected R2 challenger with their calibration fits, not two candidates selected by final-test results. Continuous heads without calibration can use fewer fits; the cap does not require unnecessary maps. Failure retires that test as selection evidence for subsequent tuning; it remains an honest reported failed result. A replacement final test must contain new later mature outcomes or an independently justified untouched partition declared before access. Renaming the experiment or choosing a new metric does not make reused outcomes unseen. All configurations, seeds, detector choices, feature sets, cancelled/failed jobs, analyst decisions and test accesses go in the trial registry, including those producing no favourable result. Selection over many backtests is itself a source of overfitting. Bailey and colleagues.

Compute proposal: two campaigns per month maximum, one declared task×horizon×market/kind scope per campaign; at most 24 declared learned-configuration alternatives (six initially) and four outer folds, within 96 total learned fits per campaign and192permonth acrossdev/prod, including regularised numerical baselines, inner search, outer/final refits and calibrators. The24-alternative catalogue ceiling does not promise every configuration a full set of fits; the96-fit cap dominates and the evaluated schedule is predeclared before outer results. The initial scalar-target illustration uses six configurations × three inner splits × four outer folds = 72 fits, plus eight outer refits, eight calibrators and four final refit/calibration fits: 92 total. Extra event/quantile heads, seeds and ablations consume the remaining cap or a separate admitted campaign. This is a total ceiling, not 24×4×unbounded inner splits. At most three sequential CPU jobs per campaign, two vCPUs and two hours per job,4GiB process working-set target,2GiB inputs and100MiB inference artefact. The cost lane identifies ml.m5.large as a candidate with two vCPUs and8GiB actual instance memory; the whole instance is billed. Four GiB is not a fictitious requested instance size. These sizing bounds require runtime/dependency evidence. This permits at most six billable instance-hours per campaign and twelve per month, subject to the money gate. Abort rather than exceed either resource or admitted GBP budget. No always-on endpoint. Seven horizon definitions do not authorise seven monthly campaigns. Python Durable Functions coordinates jobs; approved portable Lambda inference and stored forecasts follow later proof. The two-campaign limit applies across dev/prod for the whole project. The £100 all-in ceiling, £70 operating allowance and £30 reserve dominate these caps; shared $200 credit status is unknown, and no uncovered paid fallback is allowed.

The partition manifest records four distinct boundaries for every fold: estimator-fit end, calibration origin start/end, and validation origin start/end, plus outcome availability deadlines. First remove ineligible origins and labels using the frozen task and source manifest. Next construct date blocks without separating assets from the same date. Then purge the estimator-fit labels against calibration, and calibration labels against validation. Only after those exclusions may the inner search fit transformations and estimators. A calibration row needs both its original out-of-fold or frozen-estimator prediction and its matured label; its feature timestamp must still respect that row’s own historical cutoff. The resulting eligible rows and exclusions are persisted, so an apparent reduction in sample size is explained rather than recovered by weakening the boundary.

For example, if a validation block starts 1 July 2026, a twelve-month label originating December 2025 is not a training example for that fold: its terminal outcome is still in the future. A January 2025 label that matured January 2026 might be usable in calibration, but only if its prediction came from an estimator fitted without that row or future observations, its publication lag was satisfied, and its full interval obeys the earlier calibration boundary. The same row may become eligible in a later expanding fold once its outcome is known. That is a new fit with a new manifest, not permission to rewrite what an earlier model knew.

Deterministic input failures are resolved before statistical comparisons. A missing interior path observation blocks path-risk truth; an evidenced terminal observation may still support the return task. Missing benchmark data blocks only the excess task unless the model’s approved feature contract itself requires that benchmark. An ambiguous split blocks total-return construction. A model depending on unavailable news cannot quietly substitute zero sentiment. A separately qualified no-news artefact can serve only if already approved for the relevant scope. An unresolved merge or delisting remains in the dataset manifest with its exclusion reason and outcome status, exposing survivorship rather than hiding it.

A campaign’s pre-registration names the exact scalar target and heads it will fit. It must state whether the six illustrated configurations concern a terminal-return estimator, one event classifier or one quantile head. The 92-fit schedule cannot be reused as the cost of an entire distribution plus three risk events. All target heads, calibration maps, optional factors, retries that actually refit, markets and environments count against the same project limits. Baseline computations and data preparation also consume wall-clock and money even when they are not learned fits. Budget admission therefore uses the whole measured campaign, while the fit registry explains the scientific search. If only baselines can be admitted within the budget, that is an explicit baseline stage across retained horizons, with unavailable probabilities where support is absent.

SCI-06 · Metrics, dependence and incremental value

Predeclare Brier as primary binary-event loss, log loss as secondary, and reliability diagrams with event counts and uncertainty. Brier=(p−y)^2; log loss=−[y ln p+(1−y)ln(1−p)]. A numerical epsilon, proposed 10^-6 for finite reporting, must be disclosed and raw invalid zero/one contradictions retained. Lower Brier is not proof of improved calibration alone; inspect reliability separately. Direction accuracy is descriptive and is not the selection objective. Compare against trailing event rate and rule-probability variants only if qualified.

For return distributions predeclare average pinball loss over q10/q50/q90, empirical central-80% coverage, interval width and median MAE. CRPS may be added only when a coherent full CDF is emitted. For risk events use the same proper-score/coverage approach on their distinct ground truth. Report unconditional and per-market/kind/horizon/regime performance; pooled improvement cannot authorise an unsupported India NAV subgroup. Abstention coverage and losses conditional on issuance must accompany all headline scores, preventing a model from looking better by silently avoiding difficult cases.

Use paired candidate-minus-baseline losses on exactly matched origins. Proposed uncertainty uses 2,000 moving-block bootstrap replicates of whole cross-asset date blocks, with block duration at least the task label horizon and sensitivity at one and two times that duration. This is a dependence-aware diagnostic, not an independence guarantee or an implemented backtest. Report date count, instruments, overlapping labels, non-overlapping temporal blocks and sensitivity. A ten-year daily panel does not supply 2,500 independent yearly outcomes; correlated tickers cannot multiply ten yearly market episodes into hundreds of independent regimes. If fewer than 20 non-overlapping blocks are available, the proposed policy calls uncertainty exploratory and forbids an automatic evidence claim; this is a conservative disclosure floor, not a promotion minimum or statistical theorem.

Predeclare practical effect and uncertainty together. A proposed candidate test asks for at least 2% relative primary-loss reduction against the strongest eligible simple baseline and a paired 95% block interval favouring improvement, with no material subgroup deterioration. The 2% is a provisional complexity/cost hurdle requiring owner approval and pilot precision analysis, not a forecast accuracy guarantee. Proposed central-80% coverage tolerance is ±5 percentage points overall, with uncertainty and per-subgroup reliability, never a single point estimate pass. If precision cannot support those distinctions, remain in shadow or baseline-only. Thresholds must be revised before test access using declared pilot/training-only evidence and the owner’s usefulness preference, not tuned to pass held-out results. Predeclare one primary confirmatory claim per campaign. If multiple claims jointly justify a release, declare their family before testing and propose simultaneous Bonferroni interval levels1−0.05/m for m claims, reporting every result. Dependence-aware bootstrap intervals remain approximate; this adjustment is not an exact finite-sample guarantee. Secondary metrics diagnose failure and never become a post-hoc replacement primary objective.

Ablations compare R0, numerical R1/R2, plus patterns, vintage news, LLM factors and combinations using the same dates, task and folds. Each feature block consumes trial capacity; incremental value includes calibration, stability, missing-source resilience, inference latency, rights and marginal cash cost. A factor that merely improves extraction language cannot qualify predictive value. Conformal methods have procedure-specific guarantees and assumptions; the cited adaptive research does not confer per-instrument conditional coverage on arbitrary MP intervals. Gibbs and Candès.

F7 simulations compare owner-approved hypothetical contribution policies with do-nothing/current allocation, fixed-date benchmark contributions and a simple fixed-allocation policy. Match flow dates, currency, starting value, dividends, fees and any proposed slippage assumptions. Show gross and cost-adjusted results separately. No tax assumptions, trades or owner action are fabricated. A return forecast's scientific score and a contribution policy's realised/simulated wealth result are separate records. Financial lane XIRR remains historical money-weighted performance, never P(up).

SCI-07 · Eligibility, promotion and failure handling

Eligibility is task×artefact×market/kind×as-of specific: rights, identity, data freshness, point-in-time quality, history, features, calibration, maturity evidence, runtime compatibility and budget all have explicit pass/fail/unknown reasons. Unknown required rights fails issuance. Market unavailable, insufficient_history, no_clear_view, stale_input, incomplete_path, benchmark_unavailable, cost_paused and model_paused have different reason codes. For each of all seven horizons, retain a state-bearing output: eligible transparent baseline if scientifically supported, rules with heuristic strength explicitly labelled, in_practice for an unqualified challenger, or unavailable/pending with reasons. A long-horizon model awaiting mature evidence does not remove that horizon or receive fabricated probabilities. Return, risk and benchmark outputs can abstain independently; an available R must not imply an available P(D≥delta). Never renormalise missing growth/protection/benchmark components silently. Their owner weights 35/40/25 belong to a versioned financial decision policy, not probabilistic calibration.

The state machine is candidate → offline_qualified → shadow → owner_approved → probation → active. Any issuing stage can append paused or rolled_back; a failed candidate becomes rejected. Offline qualification freezes manifests, protocol, complete trial counts and final-test evidence. Shadow writes immutable prospective predictions without action authority; maturity is horizon-specific. Twelve-month shadow outcomes require twelve months plus publication lag before even the first origin can be scored. Eight weeks is useful operational evidence but cannot validate twelve-month accuracy.

Owner approval binds exact artefact, calibrator, tasks, eligibility scope, evidence hash, limits, expiry and previous safe version. A tuner can create a new candidate; it cannot promote itself or change live calibration. The owner may separately preapprove narrow safety-disable rules (for invalid artefact hash, revoked rights, corrupt inputs, missing data or budget ceiling), allowing an automatic pause without granting automatic promotion. Statistical degradation alerts are distinct from deterministic safety failures; their proposed trigger requires predeclared rolling mature-outcome windows and dependence-aware evidence. Resume after pause follows approved policy and owner authority; rollback only to an eligible approved version. An old version with revoked rights is not safe fallback.

Proof payloads retain both rules and ML outputs, disagreement, forecast/scenario label, origin/cutoff/issue/maturity, target/currency/basis, all versions, evidence limitations, mature outcome counts, baseline deltas and coverage. Corrections annotate previous results. These are information contracts, not screen designs.
