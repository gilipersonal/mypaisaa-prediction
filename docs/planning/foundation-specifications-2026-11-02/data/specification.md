# Market data contracts and eligibility

This is a proposed specification for MP-10, linked to MP-20, MP-27 and MP-29. ARC-01 selects Yahoo/Twelve Data Grow as preferred production candidates and considers Basic for development. It approves neither a subscription nor use rights. The full architecture remains unapproved. The contracts below make a blocked listing explainable and testable without fabricating coverage. No provider request, account check or import of personal holdings was performed.

**DATA-01 — Catalogue identity.** A security, fund share class and exchange listing have separate identities. `listing_id` is stable within MP; the identity tuple includes security identifier where independently verified, MIC, local symbol, quote unit, settlement currency and timezone. A provider symbol is a versioned mapping, with effective dates and mapping evidence, never a primary key. A currency suffix or company-name similarity cannot resolve a listing. An ADR, London ordinary share and another exchange's line require separate records. Funds additionally require scheme/share-class, accumulation/distribution and direct/regular-plan identity. Missing ISIN does not justify buying a paid identifier add-on: retain the verified local identifier and unresolved cross-reference.

The `SourceCapability` record covers quote, daily OHLCV, historical bars, NAV, splits, distributions, FX, fundamentals, dated exposures and news separately. Its key is `(source, plan, listing_id, endpoint, data_kind, environment)`. It records verified frequency, delay, history range, adjustment mode, timezone, weight per symbol, entitlement reference and last proof time. `candidate` is not `supported`; an HTTP success would establish a response, not licence permission or correct identity. A catalogue snapshot includes the configuration version and source inventory hash. Changing mappings creates a new version and invalidates affected caches/datasets.

**DATA-02 — Per-use entitlement.** Before a capability becomes eligible, evaluate six independent uses: storage/backup, training/evaluation, non-display inference/monitoring, internal raw display, derived display/export and transmission to Anthropic. The structured matrix in `catalogue.json` contains one row for each real listing and candidate source/plan. Every live use currently remains `unknown`. A grant must name listing or documented exchange scope, data kind, environment, authorised user, retention, attribution, territorial restrictions, term and primary evidence. A generic plan name never implies all six grants. A grant for daily bars cannot enable live quotes or news redistribution.

Current public [Twelve Data pricing](https://twelvedata.com/pricing) labels Basic internal non-display usage and Grow internal display access. These are distinct advertised uses; Basic is not automatically prohibited from numerical development, and Grow is not automatically licensed for ML. Its [terms](https://twelvedata.com/terms) make storage periods, tier restrictions and third-party terms material. Ask the provider to confirm the exact proposed uses, including trained artefacts, derived probabilities and a contracted external LLM processor. Keep the response and relevant agreement/version as evidence. No support message is sent by this task. The [yfinance project](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance) distinguishes its software from Yahoo data rights; its open-source software licence does not grant Yahoo or exchange data rights. Yahoo terms could not be fetched successfully in this research pass. Yahoo production grants therefore remain unverified.

**DATA-03 — Observation and time.** `contracts.json` gives required types for observations, rights evidence and quota reservations. Decimal values travel as strings. Every observation preserves `economic_at` (the price/bar's economic time), `effective_date` (especially NAV valuation date), provider `published_at` if available, `ingested_at`, conservative `known_at`, publication evidence class, source revision, normaliser version and content hash. `known_at >= ingested_at` and, when present, `known_at >= published_at`. A newly downloaded ten-year history is known to this system now. It cannot become a ten-year point-in-time backtest merely by replacing ingestion time with bar dates. An archived publication timestamp can support a separately labelled historical replay, with its evidence and limitations retained.

Forecast input eligibility requires `known_at <= origin_cutoff`; outcome evaluation can consume subsequently known outcome vintages while retaining the forecast's original input manifest. Later corrections append observations with `supersedes`; they do not rewrite forecasts or the originally observed outcome. Distinguish as-reported and restated evaluation datasets. A source disagreement becomes a quality incident with both hashes retained within permitted retention, not an average silently substituted as truth.

**DATA-04 — Units, FX and actions.** `quote_unit=GBX`, `currency=GBP`, `unit_factor=0.01` means 125.00 pence equals GBP 1.2500, exactly once. GBX is a unit code, not a separate ISO currency or a foreign-exchange pair. FX uses base local currency, counter GBP and `rate = GBP per 1 local unit`; local value multiplied by this rate gives GBP. The inverse requires an explicit transformation. A triangulated rate pins both leg IDs/times, formula and rounding. A currency hedge or fund trading currency is not inferred from the domicile.

Raw tradable prices plus actual cash distributions serve ledger valuation. Split-adjusted features and self-financing total-return label series are separately named/versioned transforms; a total-return series never has cash dividends added again. Corporate-action records distinguish announcement/known time, ex-date, record date, payment date, ratio or gross/net amount, currency, source and revision. A split effective next week cannot adjust an earlier live view merely because it is now announced. Delistings, symbol changes and suspended sessions remain in training universes to avoid survivorship bias. Missing actions cause adjusted-series ineligibility, not a zero-dividend assumption.

**DATA-05 — Price versus NAV.** OHLC requires an actual traded bar with interval boundaries, local session date, adjustment basis and optional volume. NAV has a valuation date, publication timestamp, share class and unit currency. Never create NAV open/high/low/volume, fill a missing NAV with zero, or call the prior NAV an intraday update. [AMFI's NAV publication material](https://www.amfiindia.com/uploads/Reviewoftimelimitfordisclosureof_NAV_f5ebc65062.pdf) demonstrates why valuation and publication can differ. Schedule each fund against its own verified publication policy. Pricing's Pro NAV claim does not establish Grow NAV coverage; no upgrade is implied. Exposures and fund holdings require report effective date and publication date, so an old portfolio composition is visibly dated. News has publisher/source URL, published/updated/known times, content rights, retrieval time and entity-link confidence; no news feed is selected or purchased here.

**DATA-06 — Daily origins and monitoring.** A per-listing calendar snapshot records timezone, holiday/half-day sessions, close, auction/finalisation and source version. The owner-pending `daily-origin-clocks-proposed-v2` separates four clocks after an eligible daily close: collection starts at +15 minutes; one missing-close retry starts at +40 minutes; the information cutoff freezes at +60 minutes; issuance must complete by +75 minutes and strictly before the next eligible session opens. A collected observation is admissible only if its conservative `known_at` is no later than the frozen cutoff; positive collection/validation latency is included. A retry that completes late cannot move that cutoff. If the calendar leaves no such issuance window or source/processing evidence cannot support it, abstain with `unsupported_schedule` rather than shifting clocks. The retry uses the existing bounded daily acquisition/retry allocation; it adds no quote stream, extra paid quota or intraday forecast. Those are MP operating proposals, not claims of exchange publication guarantees. Generate one immutable daily origin only after required close/actions/FX eligibility, and otherwise publish `waiting_data` or `abstained`. A London 07:30 briefing reads stored latest eligible origins; it does not pretend US and India closed simultaneously. Forecast task horizons and their calendar rules are owned by the forecasting specification.

Thirty-minute monitoring is a separate schedule during each eligible listing's regular session, starting at the next half-hour boundary after open. The gate checks actual endpoint cadence/delay, rights, calendar, freshness, credit and cash reservation. A delayed eligible quote is labelled delayed. A daily-only listing gets daily status; NAV gets publication monitoring. These observations do not regenerate every forecast horizon. Holidays, different DST transition weeks and half-days are resolved from versioned local calendars, never fixed UTC offsets. Proposed freshness is quote interval end plus documented delay plus 5 minutes; final daily close is fresh for that expected session until the next expected close plus grace; NAV becomes overdue after its verified publication deadline plus 60 minutes. Missing publication policy remains `policy_pending`. FX for close valuation pins a known observation at or before the valuation cutoff, with both age and pairing method; proposed maximum is 24 hours during expected FX sessions, separately flagged across weekends. The owner must approve these tolerances before use.

**DATA-07 — Quota and fetch discipline.** [Twelve Data credits](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5615854-credits) are weighted by endpoint and symbols, with Basic 8/minute, 800/day and 00:00 UTC reset. Reserve estimated worst-case weighted credits atomically against both buckets before dispatch, shared across all MP environments using the same provider account. Proposed Basic scheduler allocation is 640/day, manual diagnostics 80/day and incident/retries 80/day; normal workers use at most 6/minute, leaving 2/minute for recovery. These are subdivisions of 800, not extra credits. Reconcile provider-used/left headers without increasing quota on an untrusted or stale response. Unknown billing after a timeout keeps the reservation until reconciliation/reset.

Twenty eligible symbols checked at 16 slots need 320 credits/day at weight 1, plus 20 daily closes, 4 FX observations, 20 action checks and 36 retry/metadata credits = 400. This illustrative allocation is quota arithmetic, not Basic UK/India eligibility. A weight-100 fundamental call cannot fit Basic's 8/minute quota; batching does not divide its weight. A three-symbol weight-1 batch costs 3 credits, per the provider's [batch guidance](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5203360-batch-api-requests). The selected Grow credit option and actual account quota must be proven separately.

Cache keys include source/plan/entitlement version, listing, endpoint, interval, date range, timezone and adjustment mode. Proposed TTL is 25 minutes for 30-minute monitoring and 24 hours for final daily results, bounded by permitted licence retention and source freshness. Stored immutable forecasts do not rely on a cache entry surviving. Deduplicate an in-flight key so concurrent readers share one acquisition. Expired rights make cached data unavailable for that use even before object deletion completes. Negative coverage responses cache for 24 hours; authentication/rights errors open a circuit and require evidence-based correction, not credential retries.

For transport errors, retry at most three attempts in total with jittered 2/8-second waits, respecting provider `Retry-After`, minute reset, daily cap and job deadline. A 429 waits for the relevant bucket; daily exhaustion waits until UTC reset or expires the job. A 401/403 or unsupported listing is terminal for that attempt. Partial batches report each symbol outcome and preserve reservations for ambiguous items. Priority is rights/safety incident, missing daily close, eligible monitor, then optional backfill. Backfill cannot starve daily work. No key rotation, second account, hidden feed or paid upgrade bypasses a limit.

**DATA-08 — Lifecycle, modes and acceptance.** Capability states are `candidate -> evidence_pending -> verified -> enabled`, with `degraded`, `suspended`, `expired` and `revoked` exits; re-enabling needs current evidence and owner configuration authority. Acquisition states are `planned -> reserved -> dispatched -> received -> validated -> persisted -> eligible`; errors end in `rejected`, `quota_deferred`, `rights_blocked`, `expired` or `ambiguous`. Persisting does not alone imply eligibility. Withdrawal of a grant invalidates future jobs, cached projections, affected training inputs and model deployment eligibility; policy records deletion/retention obligations and derived-model disposition before the baseline is approved.

CI uses deterministic synthetic observations on real independently evidenced instrument identifiers. `mode=synthetic` is in every fixture, manifest and report and cannot satisfy live-provider acceptance. Development live-sample mode needs explicit activation, actual account proof and per-use rights, and cannot silently become production. Production records carry `mode=live`, provider receipt and proof age. `examples.json` has US, UK and India simulated cases, an FX case and negative cases; no example claims a personal holding. `acceptance.json` defines future executable tests and evidence requirements. Maker checks here are parsing/schema/reference/arithmetic checks only. Live coverage, licence verification and independent review remain outstanding; this is not another claim that the provider is ready.
