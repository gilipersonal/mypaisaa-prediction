**DATA-01 — Catalogue identity.** A security, fund share class and exchange listing have separate identities. `listing_id` is stable within MP; the identity tuple includes security identifier where independently verified, MIC, local symbol, quote unit, settlement currency and timezone. A provider symbol is a versioned mapping, with effective dates and mapping evidence, never a primary key. A currency suffix or company-name similarity cannot resolve a listing. An ADR, London ordinary share and another exchange's line require separate records. Funds additionally require scheme/share-class, accumulation/distribution and direct/regular-plan identity. Missing ISIN does not justify buying a paid identifier add-on: retain the verified local identifier and unresolved cross-reference.

*Universe and segments (owner, 3 Nov 2026).* A listing's catalogue membership is one or more versioned roles: `holding` (the owner holds it), `watch_only` (tracked and forecast without being held), `discovery` (in the curated discovery universe of about 150 names, scanned on end-of-day data) and `segment` (eligible for a top-5 segment). The owner-selected top-5 segments are US large caps, US tech, India Nifty 50, India midcap, ETFs (US, UK and India) and commodities (gold and silver). ETFs are ranked as one list per market (owner, 3 Nov 2026, SCI-D09: "One list per market (Recommended)"): a top 5 for US ETFs, one for UK ETFs and one for India ETFs, so the catalogue has three ETF segments (`SEG-ETF-US`, `SEG-ETF-UK`, `SEG-ETF-IN`). There is no UK FTSE top-5 segment; UK holdings and watch-only listings are still covered. Ranking within a segment uses the 35/40/25 combined score owned by the financial specification; this specification supplies only the identity, coverage and freshness each ranked listing needs. Segment membership lists, index-constituent sources and their dates are proposals to verify; an index name is not evidence of constituents. Each membership change creates a new catalogue version (DATA-08).

The `SourceCapability` record covers quote, daily OHLCV, historical bars, NAV, splits, distributions, FX, fundamentals, dated exposures and news separately. Its key is `(source, plan, listing_id, endpoint, data_kind, environment)`. It records verified frequency, delay, history range, adjustment mode, timezone, weight per symbol, entitlement reference and last proof time. `candidate` is not `supported`; an HTTP success would establish a response, not licence permission or correct identity. A catalogue snapshot includes the configuration version and source inventory hash. Changing mappings creates a new version and invalidates affected caches/datasets.

**DATA-02 — Per-use entitlement.** Before a capability becomes eligible, evaluate six independent uses: storage/backup, training/evaluation, non-display inference/monitoring, internal raw display, derived display/export and transmission to Anthropic. The structured matrix in `catalogue.json` contains one row for each real listing and candidate source/plan. Every live use currently remains `unknown`. A grant must name listing or documented exchange scope, data kind, environment, authorised user, retention, attribution, territorial restrictions, term and primary evidence. A generic plan name never implies all six grants. A grant for daily bars cannot enable live quotes or news redistribution.

Current public [Twelve Data pricing](https://twelvedata.com/pricing) labels Basic internal non-display usage and Grow internal display access. These are distinct advertised uses; Basic is not automatically prohibited from numerical development, and Grow is not automatically licensed for ML. Its [terms](https://twelvedata.com/terms) make storage periods, tier restrictions and third-party terms material. Ask the provider to confirm the exact proposed uses, including trained artefacts, derived probabilities and a contracted external LLM processor. Keep the response and relevant agreement/version as evidence. No support message is sent by this task. (Owner, 2 Nov 2026: no vendor is contacted, so these questions stay unsent; see the risk acceptance below.) The [yfinance project](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance) distinguishes its software from Yahoo data rights; its open-source software licence does not grant Yahoo or exchange data rights. Yahoo terms could not be fetched successfully in this research pass. Yahoo production grants therefore remain unverified. The later MP-10 research read the Yahoo Terms of Service (updated 1 November 2026): they forbid collecting data by automated means without express permission (RES-DATA-12), and Twelve Data's pricing card labels Grow view-only (RES-DATA-11).

**DATA-03 — Observation and time.** `contracts.json` gives required types for observations, rights evidence and quota reservations. Decimal values travel as strings. Every observation preserves `economic_at` (the price/bar's economic time), `effective_date` (especially NAV valuation date), provider `published_at` if available, `ingested_at`, conservative `known_at`, publication evidence class, source revision, normaliser version and content hash. `known_at >= ingested_at` and, when present, `known_at >= published_at`. A newly downloaded ten-year history is known to this system now. It cannot become a ten-year point-in-time backtest merely by replacing ingestion time with bar dates. An archived publication timestamp can support a separately labelled historical replay, with its evidence and limitations retained.

Forecast input eligibility requires `known_at <= origin_cutoff`; outcome evaluation can consume subsequently known outcome vintages while retaining the forecast's original input manifest. Later corrections append observations with `supersedes`; they do not rewrite forecasts or the originally observed outcome. Distinguish as-reported and restated evaluation datasets. A source disagreement becomes a quality incident with both hashes retained within permitted retention, not an average silently substituted as truth.

**DATA-04 — Units, FX and actions.** `quote_unit=GBX`, `currency=GBP`, `unit_factor=0.01` means 125.00 pence equals GBP 1.2500, exactly once. GBX is a unit code, not a separate ISO currency or a foreign-exchange pair. FX uses base local currency, counter GBP and `rate = GBP per 1 local unit`; local value multiplied by this rate gives GBP. The inverse requires an explicit transformation. A triangulated rate pins both leg IDs/times, formula and rounding. A currency hedge or fund trading currency is not inferred from the domicile.

*Gold and silver (owner, 3 Nov 2026).* Gold and silver are tracked as the spot pairs XAU/USD and XAG/USD, quoted in USD per troy ounce, not as an exchange listing, an ETF or a futures contract. Proposed identity: a spot-reference listing whose MIC is the ISO 10383 no-market code `XXXX`, with symbol `XAU/USD` or `XAG/USD`, currency and quote unit USD, unit factor 1 and the provider's stated daily-bar timezone. GBP value is the USD price multiplied by the pinned GBP-per-USD rate, applied once. A spot pair has no exchange close, so its daily origin uses the provider's daily bar boundary recorded in the calendar snapshot (DATA-06), and it has no corporate actions. Twelve Data coverage, delay and history for both pairs on Grow 55 are `live_proof_pending`; no MP research has checked them yet. The daily value was answered on 5 November 2026 (OWN-26, "Use the provider's daily bar"): the option read "Each day's value is the provider's daily bar, with its boundary recorded, once the first month proves cover." Its cost read "It rests on Twelve Data's cover and bar time, both unverified, and GBP needs a rate pinned at that time." Until the first month proves cover the pairs stay unavailable for formal forecast tasks (SCI-D08).

Raw tradable prices plus actual cash distributions serve ledger valuation. Split-adjusted features and self-financing total-return label series are separately named/versioned transforms; a total-return series never has cash dividends added again. Corporate-action records distinguish announcement/known time, ex-date, record date, payment date, ratio or gross/net amount, currency, source and revision. A split effective next week cannot adjust an earlier live view merely because it is now announced. Delistings, symbol changes and suspended sessions remain in training universes to avoid survivorship bias. Missing actions cause adjusted-series ineligibility, not a zero-dividend assumption.

**DATA-05 — Price versus NAV.** OHLC requires an actual traded bar with interval boundaries, local session date, adjustment basis and optional volume. NAV has a valuation date, publication timestamp, share class and unit currency. Never create NAV open/high/low/volume, fill a missing NAV with zero, or call the prior NAV an intraday update. [AMFI's NAV publication material](https://www.amfiindia.com/uploads/Reviewoftimelimitfordisclosureof_NAV_f5ebc65062.pdf) demonstrates why valuation and publication can differ. Schedule each fund against its own verified publication policy. Pricing's Pro NAV claim does not establish Grow NAV coverage; no upgrade is implied.

*Fund valuation sources (owner, 3 Nov 2026).* **UK funds:** in Grow 55's first paid month, test by API whether it returns NAVs for each of the owner's UK funds (OEICs and unit trusts), recording a `SourceCapability` per fund as `verified` or `unsupported` with its proof. A fund Grow 55 covers is valued from that NAV under the rules above; a fund it does not cover is valued from the owner's statements. The test buys nothing: Twelve Data's public pages disagree on which plan carries NAVs (RES-DATA-11), and failing the test never triggers an upgrade. **Indian funds:** valued from the owner's holdings statements; no AMFI file or provider NAV feed is used, and all other Indian market data comes from Twelve Data with Yahoo beside it (DATA-06). A statement-sourced valuation is an observation with `source: owner_statement`, the statement's valuation date as `effective_date` and `economic_at`, and the import's approval time as `known_at`; it reaches the ledger only through the controlled import and approval flow. It is shown with its age and is never refreshed by carrying it forward. Proposed, for the owner to confirm: a statement-valued fund enters valuation and risk views but no forecast task that needs a daily series, until a verified daily NAV source exists. Exposures and fund holdings require report effective date and publication date, so an old portfolio composition is visibly dated. News has publisher/source URL, published/updated/known times, content rights, retrieval time and entity-link confidence; no news feed is selected or purchased here.

**DATA-06 — Daily origins and monitoring.** A per-listing calendar snapshot records timezone, holiday/half-day sessions, close, auction/finalisation and source version. The owner-pending `daily-origin-clocks-proposed-v2` separates four clocks after an eligible daily close: collection starts at +15 minutes; one missing-close retry starts at +40 minutes; the information cutoff freezes at +60 minutes; issuance must complete by +75 minutes and strictly before the next eligible session opens. A collected observation is admissible only if its conservative `known_at` is no later than the frozen cutoff; positive collection/validation latency is included. A retry that completes late cannot move that cutoff. A late price is not a missed collection (ADM-06, "No, keep it apart", 5 November 2026): the option read "A late price stays a data quality incident. The +40 retry is the only automatic one, and a newer price arrives at the next check." Its cost read "A price that lands after the retry waits for the next check." If the calendar leaves no such issuance window or source/processing evidence cannot support it, abstain with `unsupported_schedule` rather than shifting clocks. The retry uses the existing bounded daily acquisition/retry allocation; it adds no quote stream, extra paid quota or intraday forecast. Those are MP operating proposals, not claims of exchange publication guarantees. Generate one immutable daily origin only after required close/actions/FX eligibility, and otherwise publish `waiting_data` or `abstained`. A London 07:30 briefing reads stored latest eligible origins; it does not pretend US and India closed simultaneously. Forecast task horizons and their calendar rules are owned by the forecasting specification.

Thirty-minute monitoring is a separate schedule during each eligible listing's regular session, starting at the next half-hour boundary after open. The gate checks actual endpoint cadence/delay, rights, calendar, freshness, credit and cash reservation. A delayed eligible quote is labelled delayed. A daily-only listing gets daily status; NAV gets publication monitoring. These observations do not regenerate every forecast horizon. *Sources (owner, 2 and 3 Nov 2026):* Grow 55 gives LSE, NSE and BSE at end of day only, so the 30-minute checks read Twelve Data quotes for US listings and Yahoo's delayed intraday prices for UK and Indian listings, each labelled with its source and delay. Daily closes come from Twelve Data; Yahoo's daily values are a cross-check, and a disagreement beyond tolerance is a quality incident (DATA-03), never an average. If Yahoo is unavailable, a UK or Indian listing shows daily status only, not a stale value presented as current. Holidays, different DST transition weeks and half-days are resolved from versioned local calendars, never fixed UTC offsets. Proposed freshness is quote interval end plus documented delay plus 5 minutes; final daily close is fresh for that expected session until the next expected close plus grace; NAV becomes overdue after its verified publication deadline plus 60 minutes. Missing publication policy remains `policy_pending`. FX for close valuation pins a known observation at or before the valuation cutoff, with both age and pairing method; proposed maximum is 24 hours during expected FX sessions, separately flagged across weekends. The owner approved these tolerances on 5 November 2026 (OWN-07, "Approve the proposed limits now"): the option read "A quote is fresh to its interval end plus its delay plus 5 minutes. A close is fresh to the next expected close plus a grace. A fund price is overdue 60 minutes after its deadline. FX may be 24 hours old." Its cost read "The grace has no figure yet. The limits may move after Grow 55's first month, which would change what shows as late." The close grace has no figure yet and stays open (DATA-DEC-02); the cache/retry rules in DATA-07 are not covered by the pick and stay proposed.

**DATA-07 — Quota and fetch discipline.** [Twelve Data credits](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5615854-credits) are weighted by endpoint and symbols, with Basic 8/minute, 800/day and 00:00 UTC reset. Reserve estimated worst-case weighted credits atomically against both buckets before dispatch, shared across all MP environments using the same provider account. Proposed Basic scheduler allocation is 640/day, manual diagnostics 80/day and incident/retries 80/day; normal workers use at most 6/minute, leaving 2/minute for recovery. These are subdivisions of 800, not extra credits. Reconcile provider-used/left headers without increasing quota on an untrusted or stale response. Unknown billing after a timeout keeps the reservation until reconciliation/reset. Grow 55 (prod, owner, 2 Nov 2026) has a 55-credit minute bucket and no daily cap; proposed normal workers use at most 45/minute, leaving 10/minute for incidents and retries. Basic (dev) and Grow 55 (prod) are different plans, so each environment reserves only against the account it uses, and prod never borrows dev credits or the reverse.

Credits are not the constraint on Grow 55: its minute rate allows about 2.4 million a month. The minute bucket is the constraint: the busiest scheduled minute is one 30-credit check slot or a close-collection batch, and 180 bars plus their retries take about 8 minutes at 45/minute inside the +15 to +60 minute window. A one-time backfill costs about 42 credits per listing (one 5,000-bar series, full dividends and splits at 20 each, one earliest-timestamp call), about 7,566 for 180 listings plus FX and commodities, or about 169 minutes at 45/minute; it runs at backfill priority, outside the daily-origin windows, over several days. If `/last_change` does not cover a MIC (an unsent vendor question), that MIC's listings fall back to a full 40-credit pull scheduled outside the daily-origin windows; a daily full pull of all 180 would be 7,200 credits and about 160 minutes, which the design avoids. Grow's history depth for LSE, NSE and BSE is unstated, so the 10-year similar-days lookback is checked in Grow 55's first month alongside the UK fund test (DATA-05).

*Adding a listing.* The marginal cost shown before the owner confirms an addition (DATA-08) is about 44 credits a month for its bar and retry, about 7 for actions, 42 once for backfill, 286 a month if it is a US listing on 30-minute checks, and 44 a month more if it lists on a MIC not yet polled for change detection. An addition that would push any scheduled minute past the normal-worker cap, or that would need a higher tier, is refused rather than upgraded; a tier change is a separate owner decision with recosting.

A weight-100 fundamental call cannot fit Basic's 8/minute quota; batching does not divide its weight. A three-symbol weight-1 batch costs 3 credits, per the provider's [batch guidance](https://support.twelvedata.com/en/articles/5203360-batch-api-requests). The Grow 55 account quota and every weight above must still be proven on the live account.

Cache keys include source/plan/entitlement version, listing, endpoint, interval, date range, timezone and adjustment mode. Proposed TTL is 25 minutes for 30-minute monitoring and 24 hours for final daily results, bounded by permitted licence retention and source freshness. Stored immutable forecasts do not rely on a cache entry surviving. Deduplicate an in-flight key so concurrent readers share one acquisition. Expired rights make cached data unavailable for that use even before object deletion completes. Negative coverage responses cache for 24 hours; authentication/rights errors open a circuit and require evidence-based correction, not credential retries.

For transport errors, retry at most three attempts in total with jittered 2/8-second waits, respecting provider `Retry-After`, minute reset, daily cap and job deadline. A 429 waits for the relevant bucket; daily exhaustion waits until UTC reset or expires the job. A 401/403 or unsupported listing is terminal for that attempt. Partial batches report each symbol outcome and preserve reservations for ambiguous items. Priority is rights/safety incident, missing daily close, eligible monitor, then optional backfill. Backfill cannot starve daily work. No key rotation, second account, hidden feed or paid upgrade bypasses a limit.

**DATA-08 — Lifecycle, modes and acceptance.** Capability states are `candidate -> evidence_pending -> verified -> enabled`, with `degraded`, `suspended`, `expired` and `revoked` exits; re-enabling needs current evidence and owner configuration authority. Acquisition states are `planned -> reserved -> dispatched -> received -> validated -> persisted -> eligible`; errors end in `rejected`, `quota_deferred`, `rights_blocked`, `expired` or `ambiguous`. Persisting does not alone imply eligibility. Withdrawal of a grant invalidates future jobs, cached projections, affected training inputs and model deployment eligibility; policy records deletion/retention obligations and derived-model disposition before the baseline is approved. For Yahoo-derived values already sent to Claude, one part was answered on 5 November 2026 (ADM-07, "Record it, keep the outputs"): the option read "MP records what was sent and says it cannot recall it. Claude text built on it stays, labelled." Its cost read "Values stay wherever Anthropic holds them. How long is unverified." The Grow 55 withdrawal treatment stays open (DATA-DEC-04).

Code then resolves the identity (DATA-01), checks coverage with the provider (Twelve Data or Yahoo) through a capability probe that is itself reserved against quota, and computes the credit cost (DATA-07) and any cash effect. The owner sees the result and taps confirm in the app before anything is added; no confirm, an unsupported listing or a failed credit check adds nothing. A confirmed addition creates a new catalogue version with the owner's approval record, and the new capability starts at `candidate` and follows the lifecycle above. Claude cannot add, enable or remove a listing, change a segment or start a backfill.

Development live-sample mode needs explicit activation, actual account proof and per-use rights, and cannot silently become production. Production records carry `mode=live`, provider receipt and proof age. `examples.json` has US, UK and India simulated cases, an FX case and negative cases; no example claims a personal holding. `acceptance.json` defines future executable tests and evidence requirements. Maker checks here are parsing/schema/reference/arithmetic checks only. Live coverage, licence verification and independent review remain outstanding; this is not another claim that the provider is ready.
