## Authority and financial record

FIN-01 is the namespace envelope. Server authentication derives owner, environment and authorisation; file content and client fields cannot choose them. Agents and extraction workers can create candidates and draft revisions. Only the controlled writer can publish owner-confirmed financial changes. Approval names the exact canonical payload, operation, account mappings, source versions, selected/excluded rows, relevant catalogue/policy versions, expected ledger revision, environment, owner and expiry. A matching hash alone cannot authenticate an approval.

FIN-02 separates decimal amounts, currencies, quantities and quote units. Proposed bounds are 28 significant digits and 12 fractional places for stored money/quantity/price, 16 fractional places for FX, with 50 significant working digits. These are engineering proposals FIN-OD-01, not owner decisions. Canonical strings have no exponent, commas, plus sign, whitespace, non-finite literals or negative zero. Source literals and declared source precision remain evidence. GBP/USD/INR are initial settlement currencies. GBX is a quote unit: `12000 GBX × 0.01 GBP/GBX = 120 GBP`, before quantity multiplication or FX. A quote already in GBP has factor 1; repeating normalisation is invalid.

Retain source-booked net cash. Validate gross, fees, deductions and net independently; do not reconstruct unknown fees as zero. Calculated values retain unrounded detail; proposed presentation rounding is half-even to currency minor units. An explicitly computed allocation rounding residual is attached to its calculation and assigned deterministically to the largest absolute component, breaking ties by stable ID. It never creates a ledger fee, income or plug transaction. Source-booked reconciliation is exact after declared unit/precision conversion; a one-minor-unit discrepancy is flagged, not silently accepted. Quantity checks use supplied precision and exact action ratios, never a money tolerance.

FIN-03 distinguishes issuer, security, share class, listing and provider alias. A fund's issuing company is not each underlying issuer; a USD share class can have GBP and USD listings. ISIN identifies the security/share class, while MIC, ticker, quote currency/unit and effective dates identify a listing. CNX1/XLON/GBP and CNDX/XLON/USD share ISIN IE00B53SZB19, verified on the issuer page; keep two listing records. This public example does not identify the owner's unknown ETF. [Issuer listing table](https://www.ishares.com/uk/individual/en/products/253741/ishares-nasdaq-100-ucits-etf?locale=en_GB). The canonical DATA listing IDs are `UK-VOD-XLON-GBX`, `US-AAPL-XNAS-USD` and `IN-RELIANCE-XNSE-INR`; `contract-catalogue.json` maps their public MIC/ticker/ISIN identities to worked shorthand. All fixture balances, events and prices are fictional. FIN-04 accounts hold wrapper/country/institution aliases and permitted currencies as source-backed metadata, with no inferred tax treatment or broker credentials.

FIN-05 events are append-only economic facts, never orders. Each has economic date/precision, optional trade and settlement dates, source sequence, recorded time, cash/quantity legs, fee/deduction detail and provenance. Proposed accounting FIN-OD-03 recognises exposure on trade date and cash receivable/payable until documented settlement; settled and unsettled cash are separate components of NAV, never added twice. A linked settlement event posts equal/opposite cash-state legs: for a purchase, decrease settled cash and extinguish the negative payable by the same amount. It changes neither total NAV nor external flows, and never charges the fee twice. Missing settlement timing remains unknown. Negative balances or oversells are retained as exceptions and block unsupported calculations; margin/short support needs another contract. Ratios for splits preserve aggregate analytical basis; fractional cash in lieu needs evidence.

## Intake, exact approval and visible publication

FIN-06 receipt records bind an immutable original version/checksum before parsing. CSV, Excel, PDF, screenshots and manual entry all use this pipeline in v1. A supported-layout register qualifies specific sample-backed families; it does not claim support for every broker. Evidence locators are row/column, sheet/cell, PDF page/region or image region. Factsheets never establish ownership. Byte/type/archive/formula/page/pixel/runtime checks precede extraction; untrusted content has no tool or writer authority. Actual owner files remain deferred.

State transitions are defined in `policies.json`. A source proceeds received → quarantined → extracting → extracted, or validation/extraction failure/cancellation. An import proceeds draft → needs_resolution or review_ready → confirmed → staged → published, with failed/cancelled exits before publication. Failure includes a retry disposition; published history cannot become cancelled. A superseding correction is a separate draft/approval/commit, linked to the old result. Edits, reprocessing, remapping, row exclusions or any financially relevant policy change create a new immutable draft revision and invalidate the pending approval. A consistent subset is permitted only through explicit excluded rows and a new preview/hash; no implicit row dropping.

FIN-07 and FIN-08 bind the draft and owner approval. Proposed expiry is 15 minutes, rechecked by the writer against server time and action-specific authentication assurance. Expiry, revocation, cancellation, wrong environment or stale ledger revision denies activation. A retry of an already published operation returns its receipt even after approval expires, because that read creates no new financial effect. An unfinished expired operation needs a renewed approval for the unchanged/new preview before activation. An unchanged-payload renewal rebinds the manifest approval reference and advances its fence through a conditional recovery transition, retires the old executable seal, and verifies/seals the same immutable content again; it cannot reuse the old complete marker. Preserve previous approval/seal evidence. A changed payload requires a new draft and operation key, with the old unfinished operation cancelled. Expiry is checked using trusted writer time immediately before each activation submission; DynamoDB has no transaction server-clock predicate. A submission before expiry can complete after it, so report submission and completion times. Strict commit-before-expiry semantics would require a separate proved design and owner decision, rather than an unsupported guarantee. Sensitive-action assurance policy remains coordinated with the wider security specification; 15 minutes is FIN-OD-02.

FIN-09–12 define publication. DynamoDB transactions allow at most 100 actions and 4 MB aggregate items, with an individual item ceiling of 400 KB; the service idempotency token lasts 10 minutes. Multiple actions cannot target one item in the same transaction. These service limits include audit, receipt, metadata and dedup work. [AWS transaction API](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/APIReference/API_TransactWriteItems.html). Application business idempotency therefore outlives that token: scoped operation key → canonical payload hash → durable manifest and receipt, retained for the ledger lifecycle. Equal key/equal payload returns the original outcome; equal key/different payload is `IDEMPOTENCY_CONFLICT`. Byte duplicate, source-row duplicate and economic-event duplicate are separate checks. Distinct broker references preserve genuinely identical same-day trades; a reference-free collision needs review.

The proposed correctness reference uses one owner/environment linear ledger head. It serialises publication, including multi-account transfers/corrections. Each publication increments the revision exactly once. Parallel extraction/staging is permitted; competing activation against the same revision yields one winner, and the loser must refresh and obtain new approval. Physical table count, partition and index keys remain deliberately undecided until the access-pattern proof and cost measurements pass. Manifest headers and individual chunk receipts must themselves fit 400 KB. A large chunk inventory uses immutable paged manifest children with a root digest and exact page/count bounds; the full ordered digest covers every page. The header does not accumulate an unbounded chunk array.

1. Reserve a stable operation/manifest ID using a conditional write; record expected revision and monotonically increasing fencing token. Fences never reset after lease expiry. A resumed worker acquires a new fence. Financial staging records remain invisible.
2. Stage immutable chunks containing events, event-level audit and `EventIntent` records. An intent contains stable scoped commit/message IDs, payload reference and deduplication key; it contains no root digest or delivery state. Each bounded chunk transaction checks the active fence/state and writes chunk completion plus hashes/counts. Validate both action count and serialized item-byte budget before submission. No chunk overwrites another version; a stale worker cannot mark completion. Required source objects already exist and have verified checksum/version references.
3. Stop new chunk writes through a conditional state transition from staged/open to sealing. Strongly read all declared chunk receipts and their immutable content; verify exact row/event/audit/intent count, ordering and digest, with no undeclared chunk. Seal as complete only for the same fence and expected hash. Referenced items cannot mutate after sealing. A crash before completion is resumable or cancellable, never visible.
4. In one bounded `TransactWriteItems`, conditionally advance the head from expected to resulting revision; change the complete manifest to published; finalise durable idempotency/receipt; consume the exact approval; append activation audit and the single publication outbox pointer. Preconditions include the same fence, immutable complete digest, expiry/assurance/revocation and expected revision. The receipt can be co-located with the business-operation record, but its fields remain required. Do not double-count separate condition checks on an item already updated conditionally.
5. Create the separate `PublicationPointer` with the completed manifest root in the final transaction, then dispatch it only after confirming publication. The pointer is outside the content graph and its immutable manifest enumerates all event-level audit and notification obligations. Delivery is at least once, with stable message IDs and consumer deduplication. Mutable `DeliveryState` and append-only `DeliveryAttempt` records are separate from sealed intents and join by owner/environment, commit and message IDs. Failure, retry and success update those delivery records, never any intent, chunk/page receipt or sealed root. Replay can rebuild pointer/delivery work from committed evidence.

`hash-contract.json` defines the exact versioned FIN-HASH-1 canonical projections and encodings. Compute immutable leaf digests, ordered `ChunkContent`, optional ordered manifest pages, then the manifest root. Event intents never reference that root. Own digest fields are excluded explicitly; manifest activation fields `state`, `resulting_revision` and `published_at` are outside its content digest and remain protected by the atomic publication protocol. The fence and approval reference are included, so recovery creates a new sealed root. Unknown fields/versions are refused. Page refs bind both page ID and digest. `hash-graph-example.json` is a small synthetic graph with computed hashes; `verify_hash_contract.py` independently verifies it and proves leaf-mutation detection plus delivery-state independence. The earlier `contract-examples.json` digest placeholders remain shape examples and provide no content proof. This offline document helper is not backend implementation or cloud evidence.

A small import may put events, audit, head, approval, idempotency, receipt and outbox in one transaction if the measured budget fits, preserving the same semantics. Large imports never publish chunk by chunk. All API reads, agents, projections, calculators, exports, replay and outbox consumers first pin a strongly read published head and follow its committed manifest ancestry; staging is excluded. A pinned old revision remains valid while a newer one publishes. Pagination cursors bind that revision. Index discovery can lag: resolve completeness from manifests and authoritative base items. Missing expected data yields `INTEGRITY_INCOMPLETE`, not a partial success. GSIs and Streams are eventually consistent and cannot certify complete publication. [AWS read consistency](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/amazondynamodb/latest/developerguide/HowItWorks.ReadConsistency.html).

A lost response is `outcome_unknown`, not failure: strongly read the business receipt/manifest/head and retry only the same operation. Never issue a compensating import merely because a timeout occurred. Retryable throttling/conflicts use bounded backoff/jitter and persist attempts. Conditional revision/hash/expiry failures require refresh rather than blind retries. Cancellation can win only before publication, conditional on manifest state/fence; after publication, use correction.

S3 and DynamoDB have no shared transaction. An object upload cannot commit a portfolio. Reconciliation compares scoped object/version/checksum receipts to upload intents: an object without a record is quarantined as an orphan; a record with a missing/wrong object is blocked; duplicate notifications resolve the same receipt. S3 notifications can repeat. [AWS S3 notifications](https://docs.aws.amazon.com/AmazonS3/latest/userguide/EventNotifications.html). An orphan is not automatically deleted. Cleanup follows the approved retention/deletion policy with a recorded disposition. Who records it was answered on 5 November 2026 (ADM-20, "Code cancels, orphans yours"): the option read "Only code cancels a run. You record what happens to each orphan, as drawn." Private encryption, least-privilege access and denied public access apply; required business audit is unsampled. Monitoring and access-event retention/cost join the controls/cost lane.

## Corrections, missing history and audit queries

A correction commits a linked inverse of the original event and its replacement, atomically visible together. The original persists. Replay sums original, inverse and replacement; it must not also exclude the original by a supersedes flag. The inverse negates every original cash/quantity leg, fee and accounting effect, not just the headline amount. A reversal without replacement is a separate operation. Dependency analysis checks later sales/actions/transfers/baselines and rejects an inconsistent reversal or includes the necessary approved corrective set. Two corrections to the same original revision cannot both win. Backdated corrections produce a new recorded revision and restated historical views, while issued metrics/forecasts retain their original input versions.

FIN-13 observations record known quantity/value/basis independently. A snapshot supplies no invented buy/deposit/date. Later backfill creates an owner-confirmed rebase: retire the old opening baseline at the explicit cutover in the new projection generation, replay accepted history once, reconcile overlapping observations and preserve prior generations. Observations are assertions alongside derived positions, not additive transactions. Unexplained differences stay open; no plug trades. A rebuild needs the whole history (OWN-19, "Whole history only", 5 November 2026): the option read "A rebuild can be approved only when statements cover every unit. A part one stays held and visible." An open cash difference (OWN-31, "Withhold only if material"): the option read "A small difference leaves the return showing and flagged. Above a limit you approve, the return is withheld." The limit is not set yet and goes with the reconciliation limits (FIN-OD-01). Coverage is per account/time/field. Missing fees can block net return while current valuation remains available.

FIN-16 audit needs these access patterns: scoped operation/idempotency outcome; all source/draft/approval/commit joins; account/listing event history by economic date with recorded revision; all recorded changes across a period; complete event set by commit/chunk; pending/failed manifests and S3 receipt exceptions; supersession/dependency chain; financial head and projection generation; unsent outbox work and delivery attempts; metric/forecast input lineage; privileged/denied access and export/deletion events. Economic-date and recorded-time queries are distinct. Required index designs must support bounded pagination, owner/environment isolation and revision filtering. A forensic owner query such as “who changed this fee, from which document, what did I approve, and which forecasts used it?” must resolve without general table scanning. Stale-index discovery cannot omit mandatory lineage: authoritative manifest joins remain available.

## Historical return and valuation contracts

FIN-14 MetricResult carries value or null, unit, status, method/policy versions, scope/boundary, start/end, ledger revision, source/price/FX/action/valuation versions, coverage, uncertainty and reasons. `available`, `partial`, `unavailable`, `ambiguous`, `nonconvergent` and `integrity_failed` are distinct. Unknown never becomes zero or a neutral ranking score. Status is computed before UI/agent wording. Rights must permit the specific storage/derivation/display use; an otherwise fresh source with unknown entitlement is ineligible. Known time means no earlier than both publication and application availability/ingestion; historical vintage reconstruction is separately labelled.

NAV at a common valuation cutoff is cash (settled plus separately classified receivable/payable) plus units × normalized unadjusted price × dated FX, without double-counting unsettled trade cash. Quote/NAV economic time, publication/known time and ingestion time remain distinct. A date-only source is not assigned a fabricated intraday time. Latest-eligible observations must satisfy a versioned market/calendar/freshness policy; calendar closure differs from an unexplained missing quote. Price and FX cutoffs and ages are disclosed. Missing a component gives a known subtotal, unknown residual and withheld complete NAV, even when the omitted weight cannot be measured.

Investor-perspective XIRR uses `XNPV(r) = Σ C_i / (1+r)^((d_i-d_0)/365)`, real `r > -1`, actual whole calendar days and one currency. Contributions into the measured investment are negative; distributions out and terminal valuation are positive. Normalize dates then aggregate same-day cash flows; require at least two distinct dates, nonzero dated span and both signs. Annualisation is explicitly labelled even below one year. Excel's XIRR is related to XNPV at zero and can fail iteratively; MP must additionally expose multiple-root ambiguity instead of depending on a guess. [Microsoft XIRR](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/excel/functions/xirr-function), [Microsoft XNPV](https://support.microsoft.com/en-gb/excel/functions/xnpv-function).

At holding scope, acquisitions plus attributable costs are negative; sale proceeds net of costs and net cash distributions are positive. Linked reinvestment is a positive distribution and negative acquisition on the same date, not an extra external contribution. At account scope, only crossings of that account boundary plus terminal account NAV count. A transfer to another owned account is external to each account but cancels at portfolio scope when matched. At whole-portfolio scope, buys, sells, retained dividends, fees, taxes withheld as recorded cash facts, and internal FX are internal; they change NAV rather than adding external contributions. Unmatched transfers remain in-transit/unresolved and cannot be treated as gains. In-kind boundary transfers use independently supported date-specific fair value; unknown fair value blocks the return for that boundary. Portfolio transfer-in-transit is valued once, never once in each account.

For a period starting after inception, include negative opening fair value and label the result “since valuation date”; do not call it since-purchase return. Include one positive terminal NAV at the documented end cutoff, after same-day events. Fully liquidated scope may have zero terminal NAV but must still include proceeds; do not add sale proceeds again to cash NAV. GBP cash flows use dated execution GBP when actually booked; otherwise permitted contemporaneous reference FX produces an explicitly estimated GBP metric. Terminal FX is valuation-date FX. Unknown/missing history, unsupported fees, ambiguous dates, stale valuation or broken reconciliation withholds the dependent net XIRR.

Proposed solver contract FIN-OD-04 searches a documented bounded domain `(-0.999999,100]` with transformation `u=log(1+r)`, rate/residual tolerances, finite iteration budget and root-isolation diagnostics. A bracket grid alone cannot prove all roots, especially tangencies. One accepted rate is allowed only for a conventional aggregated sign pattern with uniqueness established, or a certified unique root in the declared domain with no unsupported outside-domain claim. Nonconventional flows without a uniqueness certificate return ambiguous/unsupported, even if one numerical root is found. How such a found rate is shown was answered on 5 November 2026 (OWN-14, "Show it, not picked"): the option read "The found rate appears in the note beside the status, never as the headline." The headline stays null; the solver policy itself stays open (FIN-OD-04). Multiple verified roots return all candidates and null headline rate. No root in the bounded search is `no_root_in_domain`, not proof that no mathematical root exists. Zero-duration/all-zero/one-sign schedules are invalid. Residual acceptance is proposed `max(0.00000001 currency units, 10^-12 × Σ|C_i|)`; rate repeatability tolerance `10^-10`. These are numerical tolerances, not source reconciliation tolerances.

Cost basis is an accounting fact/method, XIRR is a money-weighted rate, and forecasts are conditional estimates. Keep them separate. Store source lot/basis and provenance; proposed analytical FIFO may be used only after FIN-OD-05 approval, explicitly not as tax basis. Realised gain = net disposal proceeds minus allocated analytical basis; unrealised gain = current fair value minus remaining basis. Missing basis makes basis loss unavailable without invalidating known quantity/value. Income already reflected in ledger cash must not be re-added to adjusted total-return prices. A forecast/benchmark total-return series can include reinvested distributions; it cannot drive raw ledger position valuations.

Exact TWR requires reliable valuations immediately before and after every external boundary flow (or documented daily timing that supports that equivalence). Compute each no-external-flow subperiod `r_j=V_end_before_nextflow/V_start_after_previousflow-1`, then `TWR=Π(1+r_j)-1`. Nonpositive denominator or missing valuation withholds TWR. MP does not silently substitute Modified Dietz or interpolated valuations; any approximation requires a separately named method and decision. GIPS provides methodological guidance on cash-flow treatment and linked subperiod returns; this app makes no GIPS compliance claim. [CFA Institute GIPS handbook](https://www.gipsstandards.org/standards/gips-standards-for-firms/gips-standards-handbook-for-firms/).

FIN-15 FX is `q_t = GBP per 1 native unit`; `V_GBP=V_native×q`. Cross rates pin both leg dates/sources; no future-known rate enters a historical cutoff. For no-flow total return, `1+R_GBP=(1+R_native)(1+R_FX)`; report local, FX and interaction `R_native×R_FX`, rather than forcing an additive two-term split. With flows use dated GBP flows or separately named attribution, not this simple decomposition. FIN-17 cash-flow-matched benchmarks simulate notional benchmark units from identical external boundary cash flows and the same dates/GBP conversion/timing policy. Use a versioned eligible total-return index level, including its dividend convention, fees assumptions and FX. Missing date levels require a disclosed approved next-session rule or withhold; no future level chosen opportunistically. Negative notional units or unsupported withdrawals withhold. This is hypothetical comparison, never broker orders or implied executed trades.

## Exposure, drawdown, ranking and exports

Peak drawdown is distinct from basis loss. For a positive flow-adjusted wealth index `W`, `DD_t=1-W_t/max_{s≤t} W_s`, maximum drawdown is the maximum DD on the stated observation window, with peak/trough dates. Raw NAV changes after deposits/withdrawals cannot define portfolio drawdown. Basis loss is `max(0,(B-V)/B)` for positive known remaining basis and identical valuation scope; a positive gain is reported separately. No basis or no supported wealth history yields unavailable. Discrete daily drawdown cannot claim an intraday maximum. Protection prioritises whole-portfolio risk before a single holding alarm, preserving both owner-selected risk views.

FIN-18 exposure weights use common-cutoff GBP value/complete positive portfolio NAV. Consolidate economic share classes/issuer joins for economic exposure while keeping listing currency effects distinct. Look-through equity exposure for issuer k is direct weight plus Σ(fund weight × dated fund issuer weight); do not add the fund wrapper again to issuer totals. Fund recursion needs cycle detection, maximum depth, non-overlapping leaves and residual. Cash/derivatives/unknown classification remains explicit. Pairwise fund overlap is Σ min(weight_ik,weight_jk) on comparable known leaves with coverage bounds; it is not an extra portfolio position. Report top issuer/sector/country/currency weights and HHI `Σw_k²` only with declared scope, mapping versions and residual; known-only HHI is partial and cannot stand for complete concentration. Where dated fund holdings come from was answered on 5 November 2026 (MP40-Q8, "Factsheets you supply"): the option read "You add dated fund factsheets through the import route, and MP shows the shares they list plus what is left unknown." Factsheet detail and use rights stay unverified, and, as the phase-story baseline already says, a factsheet cannot invent owner units, cost or purchase history.

Proposed covariance policy uses synchronized GBP total-return observations, a 126-session trailing target, at least 100 common valid observations, and a separate 252-session sensitivity where supported. Calendar alignment/close-time mismatch is disclosed; no fabricated zero return on an open-but-missing day. Sample covariance uses a common complete matrix, `1/(n-1)` denominator, current weights and `σ_p²=w'Σw`; annualisation by 252 is a declared comparison convention, not equal trading-day truth across all markets. Coverage below complete required holdings, stale exposures, unstable/indefinite covariance, insufficient paired observations or incompatible times withholds complete portfolio volatility/correlation-based action claims. These numerical defaults and validation evidence are FIN-OD-06, not owner-approved limits.

The owner-confirmed ranking weights remain growth 0.35, protection 0.40 and opportunity 0.25. `score=0.35G+0.40P+0.25O` only when each versioned normalized component is eligible; missing components cause abstention, no neutral 50 or hidden weight redistribution. Proposed component scales `[0,100]` require evidence and owner approval; this pack selects no buy/sell threshold. Ranking precedes no trade. Suggestions disclose forecast task/horizon, uncertainty, source/coverage, portfolio-risk counterfactual, constraints and component version. The owner-confirmed horizons are 1/5/10/20 trading sessions and 3/6/12 calendar months, plus 3–5-year conditional scenarios. Calendar-month anniversary targets roll to the preceding valid local session/valuation date and score only when the canonical value is available (FND-DEC-001/002); those forecast horizon rules do not change XIRR actual/365 day fractions. Historical return never becomes a forecast probability. FIN-OD-07 owns normalization, thresholds and ties. The owner's picks of 5 November 2026 settle when an action shows and what a gap does: OWN-09 ("Evidence first"), the option read "No label appears until evaluation supports the thresholds and you approve a ranking policy version."; OWN-17 ("Both parts go"), the option read "Any missing or split 3, 6 or 12-month forecast takes both growth and protection, so the name is not ranked."; OWN-20 ("No score, no action"), the option read "A holding not ranked shows no action until it can be ranked again, with the reason."; OWN-32 ("Conflict at every long horizon"), the option read "Wherever the rules and the model point different ways at 3, 6 or 12 months, that horizon is conflicted and no action is ranked there."; OWN-15 ("Any time evidence shows"), the option read "You can save an entry against the evidence on screen whenever no action is ranked, and the entry records why none was." Normalisation, comparability, tie handling and the threshold values stay open in FIN-OD-07.

Exports pin owner/environment, schema, published ledger revision, metric/source/policy versions, as-of, omitted evidence and content digest. Data export includes cash/event/quantity/native currencies, original and superseding events, approval/audit joins and missingness. Private encrypted delivery with access/creation/deletion audit is required; renewed-original retention and deletion effects remain explicit. A removed original is marked evidence-unavailable, not cryptographically certified intact. A hash chain without independently protected anchors cannot prove tamper resistance. Financial audit retention, digest anchor, restore/rebuild and export expiry need FIN-OD-08 and the controls lane; no MA job/account/history retirement is authorised.
