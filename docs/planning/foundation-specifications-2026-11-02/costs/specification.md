# Aggregate cost and admission specification

This proposed specification addresses MP-27/MP-29, FC-01, PL-10/12/13 and CT-06/13. It uses public primary prices checked on **2 November 2026** and reproducible decimal arithmetic. `model.json` contains every quantity, unit, line cost, 108 monthly run-rate variants and 216 ordinary/exception-month cash variants (the exception month is the conditional Apple fee month since the domain line was removed, owner 3 Nov 2026); `public-price-evidence.json` pins regional offer-file URLs, publication dates, hashes, SKUs and rate codes. These are estimates for an unbuilt system, not a bill or a claim of deployment readiness.

**COST-06 — Conservative admission.** Proposed admission uses an atomic reservation in the aggregate monthly ledger, scoped by owner/environment/route/job and idempotency key. Admit only when worst-case tax/FX-adjusted additional cash fits all limits; optional Claude also requires sufficient verified credit allocated through completion, regardless of cash headroom.
