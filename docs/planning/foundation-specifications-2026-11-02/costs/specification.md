# Aggregate cost and admission specification

This proposed specification addresses MP-27/MP-29, FC-01, PL-10/12/13 and CT-06/13. It uses public primary prices checked on **2 November 2026** and reproducible decimal arithmetic. `model.json` contains every quantity, unit, line cost, 108 monthly run-rate variants and 216 ordinary/exception-month cash variants (the exception month is the conditional Apple fee month since the domain line was removed, owner 3 Nov 2026); `public-price-evidence.json` pins regional offer-file URLs, publication dates, hashes, SKUs and rate codes. These are estimates for an unbuilt system, not a bill or a claim of deployment readiness.

**COST-06 — Conservative admission.** Proposed admission uses an atomic reservation in the aggregate monthly ledger, scoped by owner/environment/route/job and idempotency key. Define `encumbered_cash = settled_cash + outstanding_unreconciled_liability + future_fixed_commitments + open_reservations`, avoiding overlap when a reservation settles. `available = min(global_remaining, environment_remaining, route_remaining)`. Admit only when worst-case tax/FX-adjusted additional cash fits all limits; optional Claude also requires sufficient verified credit allocated through completion, regardless of cash headroom. A timeout/batch/retry with unknown external outcome keeps its liability. Cancellation releases only confirmed unspent liability; an AWS stop request is not proof that billing ceased.

The £70 plan must include full fixed invoices due in that month at the start of the month, including a selected annual setup/renewal charge. Cash admission uses invoice-month cash, never amortisation or a notional monthly saving pot. Unknown amount or due date blocks a new commitment until an approved conservative cash bound exists; settlements and effective cancellations replace or release the same liability without duplication. The draft establishes no actual purchase. **One exception exists** (owner, 2 Nov 2026): if the owner buys the Apple Developer Program, its due month may exceed £100 for that fee only, once a year; the full fee is reserved in that month, every other line must still fit, and no other fee or month may use the exception. **The top-5 scan** may add up to £5 a month (owner, 3 Nov 2026); it is a reserved cap inside the same aggregate month, a reservation that would cross it is refused, and its Claude work still needs verified credit or the owner's paid approval. Proposed internal allocations, subject to owner agreement, are data ≤£30, AWS ≤£22, Claude/evals ≤£10 and forecast/price uncertainty ≤£8, across both accounts. They are planning ceilings, not independently spendable pots. With the owner's selection, data is £27.84, AWS £18.64 and Claude option A £8.24; option A plus the scan at its cap is £13.24, above the £10 Claude/evals allocation, so where the scan sits among the allocations is an open question for the owner (the totals stay under £70 either way). A £75.84 fixed subscription fails the data/operation admission before optional jobs start. At £60 warn, at £75 reduce optional backfill/challengers/LLM work, at £85 pause optional work and preserve a proposed £15 emergency window for authentication, ledger reads, audit and recovery. This cannot guarantee that delayed cloud invoices stop exactly at £100; hard request/concurrency/timeout/retention caps and fixed commitments remain necessary. No autonomous feed upgrade or price-driven feature removal is allowed.
