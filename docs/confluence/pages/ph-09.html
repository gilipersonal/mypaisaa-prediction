L9-S01 · Validated market and instrument extensions

  • Use versioned MarketProfile/InstrumentKind/DataSource contracts for mainland China,Taiwan,Hong Kong and continental Europe as later scope.

  • Check venue/calendar/currency/dual-listing mappings,rights,history,delay and budget; unsupported handlers need tested code and approved deployment.

  • Each market/kind receives contract,replay,shadow and owner activation evidence; avoid projecting US-only validation across markets.

L9-S02 · Themes, segments and bounded broad-universe research

  • Version dated many-to-many theme membership and sources; distinguish marketing labels from measured economic exposures.

  • Use batched daily quantitative screening and a bounded LLM shortlist; compare themes/peers/benchmarks and holdings overlap.

  • Historical theme studies use historical constituents; expanded universe/data/model load must fit approved aggregate cost.

L9-S03 · New features, model families and horizons

  • Version FeatureSet/ModelAdapter/ForecastTask/Strategy/AgentManifest/EvaluationPack schemas and supported capability matrix.

  • New computation requires tested trusted code,resource costing,ablation/mature outcomes/shadow evidence and owner promotion.

  • No arbitrary runtime code/plugin download or self-authorized model/tool/budget expansion.

L9-S04 · Optional MA interchange and eventual retirement boundary

  • No shared live database or required MA API; any optional export/import needs an approved versioned file contract and reconciliation.

  • Before any later MA advanced-Forecaster retirement,refresh source/code/runtime inventory,prove agreed parity/history preservation,recovery and explicit cutover scope.

  • MA rules,tracking and XIRR remain; no MA page/issue/source/runtime change is authorized by this plan or its MP story mapping.
