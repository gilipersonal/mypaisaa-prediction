Proposed acceptance

L6-S01 · Discovery-first web dashboard with launch cost visibility

  • Adaptive accessible PWA opens on discovery/market view with main-dashboard costs, freshness, eligibility and separate holdings navigation.

  • Search/filter/saved watchlists include unowned candidates, peers/benchmarks/themes and impact on current overlap.

  • Use approved local design/package baseline; static Sites concept does not count as the PWA.

L6-S02 · Transparent ranked actions and disagreement

  • Show add/hold/pause/trim/rebalance with holdings/candidate distinction,reasons,uncertainty,portfolio impact and horizon.

  • Apply eligibility/abstention before the combined 35% growth/40% protection/25% opportunity ranking, with component scores and agreed normalization/tie/action bands.

  • Expose rules,ML and LLM-assisted views separately; disagreement and unavailable inputs cannot be hidden by a single score.

L6-S03 · Balanced horizon proof and scenario experience

  • Show nextday/5/20days, 3/6/12 months and separate 3–5-year scenario sections with cutoff,uncertainty,eligibility and evidence.

  • F6 side-by-side explanation, fan/range charts, regime/analog/event/pattern sheets and model/track-record proof remain accessible.

  • No-model/in-practice/disabled/stale/unavailable states work in chart and text alternatives; simple/technical modes preserve material uncertainty.

L6-S04 · Daily brief, risk alerts and decision journal

  • Action-first summary 07:30 Europe/London with per-market timestamps, priorities,risks,changes and upcoming events; quiet hours 22:00–07:00.

  • Define material/critical thresholds,weekends,cooldown,dedup and denied-push fallback; immediate means after detection using available cadence.

  • Retain in-app/chat alerts,wrap/reflection requirements and I-acted journal; no alert executes an action.

L6-S05 · Web import, audit and recovery journey joins

  • Consume detailed L1 preview/confirm/reversal/partial-history flows without diverging from canonical ledger contracts.

  • Join source→ledger→risk→forecast→action→outcome→audit with clear error/stale/permission states.

  • Cross-client deep links retrieve durable authorized state; offline mode labels cached ages and blocks unsafe writes.
