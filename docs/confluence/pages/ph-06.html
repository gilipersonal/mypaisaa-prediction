Acceptance

PH06-S01 · Holdings-first home with the top 5 per segment

Positive

  • The home view shows the owner's holdings first, with their ranges, and the top 5 per segment below (owner, 3 November 2026, MP-177).

  • Each holding shows its value in GBP with its local currency, its latest forecast range for the chosen horizon, its suggested action with reasons, and how fresh its price is.

  • Each segment shows its top 5 from the versioned ranking (PH06-S05): US large caps, US tech, India Nifty 50, India midcap, US ETFs, UK ETFs, India ETFs, and gold and silver; there is no UK FTSE segment, and UK holdings still appear.

  • Every row shows its data freshness and provider status, and the cost view is one tap away (PH06-S03).

  • The view reads one published version of holdings, forecasts and rankings, so every row agrees.

Negative

  • A listing without a complete combined score shows as not ranked with the parts it has, and a segment with fewer than five ranked listings shows fewer; nothing is filled in to make five.

  • Risk, staleness or a provider outage is never hidden behind a loading state.

  • The view says it is decision support, not advice, and offers no trade.

Recovery

  • If a read fails, the view shows the last loaded version with its age and a retry; it never mixes two versions.

  • After an SSE notice of a new version, the client re-reads durable state.

PH06-S02 · Search, filters and watchlists over the discovery universe

Positive

  • Search and filters cover holdings, watch-only listings and the curated discovery universe by name, symbol, market, segment and kind.

  • A candidate shows its peers, its benchmark and the change it would make to current overlap and concentration, computed by code.

  • Saved watchlists are versioned per owner and environment; themes appear once dated theme membership exists (PH09-S02).

Negative

  • A listing outside the catalogue is added only through the chat proposal, coverage check and owner confirm (PH05-S11); search cannot add it directly.

  • A listing with unverified rights or coverage shows as unavailable with its reason, never with made-up data.

Recovery

  • A failed watchlist save leaves the previous version and shows the failure; the retry is idempotent.

PH06-S03 · Spend and budget on the main dashboard

Positive

  • The view shows dev and prod spend for AWS, market data, and Claude and evaluations against the £70 plan (data £30, AWS £22, Claude and evaluations £15, forecast and price uncertainty £3), the £30 reserve and the £60, £75 and £85 thresholds.

  • It separates internal estimates (live), Anthropic usage (every six hours) and reconciled billing (daily), and states the lag of each.

  • The top-5 scan's Claude spend shows against its £5 cap.

  • If the owner ever joins the Apple Developer Program, its fee month shows the fee as the one allowed exception and admits optional work on the month without the fee (COST-Q4).

Negative

  • An unknown or unreconciled amount is shown as unknown, never as zero.

  • The cost view is never only in admin; it is one tap from the home view.

Recovery

  • When the cost collector fails, the view shows the last reconciled figures with their age and a stale label.

PH06-S04 · Static SvelteKit PWA shell with authenticated data and private caching

Positive

  • The shell is a prebuilt static SvelteKit PWA served by CloudFront from a private S3 origin with origin access control; private data and commands go through the authenticated FastAPI under /api/v1.

  • The service worker caches only the shell and approved non-sensitive assets; private responses are cached only as the offline policy allows and are cleared on sign-out, session expiry and account change.

  • Typed API clients are generated from FastAPI's OpenAPI schema, and every error carries a code, a message, the request_id and the trace_id.

  • First paint and time to interactive are measured against agreed budgets.

Negative

  • No financial record, secret or owner data appears in the public build or the CDN cache.

  • No SvelteKit server runtime, server session or SSR action is assumed.

  • The architecture Site in site/ does not count as the PWA.

Recovery

  • A new shell version is picked up on the next load; a client on an incompatible API version is told to refresh rather than failing silently.

  • Offline, the shell shows cached views with their ages and refuses writes.

PH06-S05 · Versioned ranking policy: eligibility, the 35/40/25 score and the top 5 per segment

Positive

  • A ranking policy version holds the component definitions, normalisation, the weights (growth 35, protection 40, opportunity 25), the tie-break and the action bands.

  • Eligibility and abstention run before combination, and the combined score exists only when all three components are eligible (POLICY-006).

  • After each daily issuance, code ranks each segment's eligible listings and stores the top 5 as a versioned view, with one list each for US, UK and India ETFs (owner, 3 November 2026).

  • Growth and protection come from the 3, 6 and 12-month forecasts and only the opportunity component uses shorter horizons; the lists are re-ranked daily after issuance (owner, 3 November 2026, MP-177).

  • Deterministic reference outputs for a synthetic universe reproduce exactly.

Negative

  • A missing component is never zero and never reweighted; that listing is not ranked.

  • Gold and silver stay unavailable for formal tasks, with a reason, until their valuation conventions are decided (SCI-D08).

  • No action band classifies a listing until normalisation, ties and thresholds are approved (FIN-OD-07).

  • A 1 to 20-session forecast never feeds the growth or protection component.

Recovery

  • A failed ranking run leaves the previous view current with its date; a re-run for the same issuance gives the same view.

  • A corrected forecast produces a new ranking version, and the earlier version stays for audit.

PH06-S06 · Suggested actions with reasons, uncertainty, portfolio impact and horizon

Positive

  • Holdings and candidates are visibly distinct; each action shows its reasons, uncertainty and horizon and the counterfactual change to concentration and risk, computed by code.

  • Each action names the policy version, components, sources and model versions behind it.

Negative

  • No action executes a trade, places an order, assumes a tax rule or changes contributions, and there is no sell-everything instruction.

  • Until FIN-OD-07's thresholds are approved, a listing shows its component evidence and no action label rather than a guessed one.

Recovery

  • When an input becomes stale or ineligible after publication, the action is marked stale with its reason until the next ranking run.

PH06-S07 · Rules, models and Claude views side by side, with the experimental contribution split

Positive

  • Rules (with their heuristic strength labelled), numerical models (with calibrated ranges) and the Claude-assisted explanation appear as separate views for the same listing and horizon.

  • Disagreement between rules and models is shown with each side's evidence.

  • The rules-versus-models contribution split is shown live from launch, labelled experimental, with no probability of its own (owner, 3 November 2026).

  • An F7 contribution policy appears, labelled experimental and as a simulation, only after it beats the simple comparator rules for 8 weeks in replay and shadow and the owner says yes.

Negative

  • An unavailable input or view shows as unavailable and is never folded into a single score.

  • The Claude view never shows a number that a code result did not supply.

  • An F7 simulation is never shown as a trade, an order or an instruction.

Recovery

  • If one view fails, the others still show, with the failed view's reason.

PH06-S08 · Forecast views for every horizon from the next day to 12 months, with every state

Positive

  • Sections cover the next day; 5, 10 and 20 trading days; and 3, 6 and 12 calendar months, each with as-of time, data cutoff, target, range or probability, calibration, model version and evidence.

  • Every number identifies its task, as-of time, source and model, and says whether it is evaluated, illustrative or a scenario.

  • Range and fan charts have text alternatives, and both the simple and the technical mode keep material uncertainty.

Negative

  • A horizon with no qualified model is never removed and never given a made-up probability; it shows the rules with their heuristic strength, in_practice, or unavailable with reasons.

  • Offline or stale data never appears as fresh analysis.

Recovery

  • When a forecast is corrected, the view shows the new version and annotates the old one; the original is never rewritten.

PH06-S09 · Proof and track record behind each forecast

Positive

  • The proof shows the dataset version, folds, baseline comparison, number of matured outcomes, coverage, eligibility and limitations, and annotates corrected outcomes.

  • Model sheets and the regime, analog, event and pattern sheets are reachable from a forecast; the analog sheet shows its matched days since 2015 and the number of cases.

  • Hit rate always appears with calibration, error, coverage, turnover and the benchmark.

Negative

  • No track record is claimed for a horizon before its outcomes mature; a 12-month record waits a year.

  • A judge or evaluation score is never shown as forecast accuracy.

  • Evidence from fewer than 20 non-overlapping blocks is labelled exploratory.

Recovery

  • If the proof fails to load, the forecast still shows with 'proof unavailable' and a retry; a revised outcome creates a new score version shown beside the old one.

PH06-S10 · Three and five year scenarios with editable assumptions

Positive

  • Downside, central and upside templates cover 3 and 5 years, with explicit return, inflation, FX, contribution, fee and dividend-treatment assumptions, computed by deterministic arithmetic (POLICY-004).

  • The owner can edit a copy of any template; edited assumptions are labelled as the owner's.

  • Scenarios sit in their own section, separate from the evaluated forecasts up to 12 months.

Negative

  • No scenario carries a probability, and 'central' never means most likely.

  • Template values are not used until the owner approves them (SCI-D06).

  • No 12-month model output is extrapolated to make a scenario.

Recovery

  • An invalid assumption is refused with its reason and the last saved assumptions are kept; approving new template values creates a new version.

PH06-S11 · The 07:30 London daily brief

Positive

  • The brief is issued at 07:30 Europe/London, moving with daylight saving, and leads with actions, then risks, changes and upcoming events.

  • It reads the stored latest eligible origin for each market and shows India, UK and US timestamps separately.

  • It is delivered in the app and announced by a neutral Web Push notice (PH07-S07); alerts held overnight arrive with it.

Negative

  • The brief never depends on a batch submitted that morning, and never presents the US and India as having closed at the same time.

  • No part of the brief executes an action.

Recovery

  • If the text route is unavailable, a deterministic brief is issued and labelled (PH05-S08); if MP itself was down at 07:30, the brief is issued on recovery, labelled late, and never sent twice.

PH06-S12 · Material risk alerts with quiet hours, cooldown and deduplication

Positive

  • A material or critical risk condition found on eligible as-of data, by the 30-minute checks or the daily run, creates one notification intent; immediate means right after detection at the cadence the data allows.

  • Quiet hours run 22:00 to 07:00 Europe/London; alerts in that window wait for the 07:30 brief unless an approved exception applies.

  • Cooldown and a dedup key group repeats into one alert, and denied push falls back to the in-app inbox (PH07-S08).

  • Investment risk alerts are a separate class from operational incidents.

Negative

  • An unavailable provider never produces an all-clear.

  • A push accepted by the push service is labelled a delivery attempt, not proof the owner read it.

  • No alert executes an action, and no alert payload carries a value or a holding.

Recovery

  • An alert detected during an outage is delivered after recovery with its detection time; a retried send reuses the dedup key so the owner sees one alert.

PH06-S13 · Decision journal: what I did and why

Positive

  • The owner records 'I acted' or 'I chose not to' against the exact action, evidence and policy version shown.

  • Entries are append-only and later joined to the matured outcomes of the forecasts they cite.

  • A weekly wrap-up lists the week's entries and their outcomes so far.

Negative

  • A journal entry never changes holdings or triggers a trade; holdings change only through an approved import.

  • Claude may summarise entries but never writes one.

Recovery

  • A failed save keeps the draft and retries idempotently; a correction is a new entry linked to the old one.

PH06-S14 · Web import journey on the canonical ledger contracts

Positive

  • The web journey covers upload (CSV, Excel, PDF, screenshot and manual entry), preview, guided edits, explicit complete-group subsets (POLICY-005), approval, commit, reversal and history rebuild proposals (POLICY-012), all on PH-01's contracts unchanged.

  • Approval needs a fresh step-up and starts the commit within 15 minutes of the exact approved content (POLICY-001).

Negative

  • The web app holds no ledger logic of its own; a changed or expired proposal needs a fresh approval.

  • An extracted draft is never shown as committed holdings.

Recovery

  • A page reload mid-import resumes from server state; a commit failure offers an idempotent retry and never a second commit.

PH06-S15 · Evidence trail from source to outcome, with deep links

Positive

  • From any figure the owner can open the chain source, ledger, risk, forecast, action, outcome and audit.

  • Deep links resolve to durable, authorised records on the web and the Apple app; chat links open the exact evidence context.

Negative

  • A deep link never rebuilds evidence afresh; it opens the stored record or says it is unavailable.

  • A link to another owner's or environment's record is refused.

  • Offline, cached records show their ages and writes are refused.

Recovery

  • A link to a superseded record opens it with a pointer to its successor; an expired session asks for sign-in and then returns to the record.
