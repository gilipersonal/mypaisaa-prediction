Acceptance

PH04-S01 · Versioned forecast tasks for all seven horizons

Positive

  • The catalogue holds the next session, 5, 10 and 20 trading sessions and 3, 6 and 12 calendar months, each with target equation, horizon unit, return currency, observation grid, origin rule, maturity roll, distribution support, event threshold, benchmark mapping and data, calendar and FX policies (SCI-01, task-catalogue.json).

  • Targets stay separate: terminal return and its quantiles, the up event, benchmark excess, peak drawdown on the daily path, adverse excursion, terminal loss and within-horizon risk events (SCI-02).

  • Month horizons use the anniversary rolled back to the preceding valid local session, with the day clamped to month end (FND-DEC-002, SCI-G08).

  • Every horizon keeps an output state even when no model qualifies: eligible baseline, rules with labelled heuristic strength, in practice, or unavailable with a reason (SCI-07-AC06).

Negative

  • Changing any task field creates a new version; a prediction cannot move to a different task.

  • Calendar months are never treated as 63, 126 or 252 sessions, and native and GBP variants are different tasks.

  • Gold and silver tasks stay unavailable_with_reason until SCI-D08 sets their valuation instant; tasks that need a threshold or benchmark stay unavailable until SCI-D02 is answered.

Recovery

  • A calendar correction appends an amendment and leaves the original maturity on earlier predictions (SCI-01-AC10).

  • A retired task version stays readable with all its predictions, outcomes and scores.

PH04-S02 · Daily forecast issuance from the frozen cutoff

Positive

  • Issuance waits for the frozen cutoff from PH03-S09 and completes by +75 minutes, strictly before the next eligible open (POLICY-002).

  • Each Prediction pins as-of, origin, data cutoff, issue time, task and horizon, target, distribution or probability, calibration, model and calibrator versions, feature and manifest hashes, code version and explanation references (AGENTS.md).

  • Only owner-approved artefacts run, on a Lambda inference worker, and outputs for holdings, watch-only listings and the ~150-name discovery universe are stored for clients to read (ARC-04).

  • Each output keeps the rules output, the model output and their disagreement, with the rules-versus-models contribution split labelled experimental and carrying no probability (SCI-OA-08).

Negative

  • A late job records missed_origin and never backdates; a deadline at or after the next open abstains with unsupported_schedule (SCI-G13).

  • Missing rights, stale input, insufficient history, a paused model and a cost pause each give their own reason code instead of an output (SCI-07).

  • No forecast is computed on an app request and no model trains at issuance.

Recovery

  • A retry of the same origin is idempotent: one Prediction per task, listing, origin and artefact.

  • If the inference worker fails, those tasks are marked failed for the origin and the last issued forecast stays visible with its date; nothing is issued after the deadline.

PH04-S03 · Outcome maturity and append-only ground truth

Positive

  • Session horizons mature strictly after the origin on the listing's frozen calendar, month horizons on the rolled anniversary; an outcome is known only once all terminal, path, action, FX and benchmark inputs are available (SCI-01, SCI-02).

  • Outcomes record canonical observations, first or amended status, event values and evidence links, under a canonical-source policy frozen before any outcome.

  • A corrected close or action appends a new Outcome linked by supersedes, and both the original and corrected scores survive (SCI-G12).

Negative

  • Nothing is scored before maturity; pending, missing_path, missing_fx, disputed_action, suspended and delisted_unresolved are never zero returns (SCI-02-AC09).

  • A delisted listing stays in the record with its outcome status, and a missing delisting price is unresolved, not zero (SCI-G14).

  • Switching sources to improve a score is refused.

Recovery

  • A late-published input moves outcome_known_at, never the economic date.

  • A failed maturity job reruns idempotently and writes no duplicate Outcome.

PH04-S04 · Three and five-year scenarios from editable assumptions

Positive

  • Three templates plus custom assumptions for 3 and 5 years, with explicit nominal return, inflation, FX, contributions, fees and dividend treatment (POLICY-004, SCI-08).

  • Paths are deterministic arithmetic, and contribution timing follows the financial lane's cash-flow conventions.

  • Template values are versioned and run only after the owner approves them.

Negative

  • No likelihood is shown, and central is never called most likely (SCI-08-AC01).

  • A scenario is never scored as a forecast and is never a 12-month model extrapolated.

  • Unapproved template values do not run.

Recovery

  • An edit creates a new assumption version; earlier scenario results keep the version they used.

  • An invalid assumption is refused with the field and the reason, and the last valid scenario stays shown.

PH04-S05 · Holder rules and indicators with labelled heuristic strength

Positive

  • The retained indicators and pillars are computed only from data known at the cutoff, with versioned definitions, lookbacks and seeds.

  • Rule outputs carry heuristic strength with that label, separate from any probability, and keep their disabled, insufficient-data and no-clear-view states.

  • A rules change (indicators, pillars, thresholds) is a new rules version that runs the 8-week shadow trial, beating the current rules, before the owner approves it (SCI-OA-04).

Negative

  • NAV series get no candle or OHLC features (SCI-04-AC01).

  • Rule strength is never shown or scored as a probability without a qualified mapping (PH04-S06).

  • A rules change never goes live without the trial and the owner's yes.

Recovery

  • Missing inputs give insufficient_data, never a default signal.

  • A faulty rules version is withdrawn by returning to the previous approved rules version, with its history kept.

PH04-S06 · Statistical baselines and the rules-to-probability mapping

Positive

  • R0 baselines are zero return, trailing event rate, constant base rate and historical empirical and block-path distributions over 252 and 756 eligible observations, with a seeded, reproducible bootstrap (SCI-04).

  • Baselines run on the same eligible dates as every candidate and are the mandatory comparators.

  • The rules-to-probability mapping (R0-RULEMAP) is fitted on the pre-trial calibration block and qualified per rules version; without a qualified mapping the rules comparison is reported as not scoreable.

Negative

  • A historical empirical forecast is never called calibrated without evidence.

  • Window choices count as tried configurations, and no window is chosen after viewing results.

  • A baseline lacking history gives insufficient_history rather than a shortened window.

Recovery

  • A rerun with the same seed and manifest reproduces identical outputs; a mismatch fails the run.

  • A failed mapping qualification leaves the rules unscored and that task's trial waiting for the owner.

PH04-S07 · Point-in-time dataset manifests and feature admission

Positive

  • A manifest freezes listing identities, contemporaneous membership, delisted and merged histories, raw inputs, actions, FX, calendars, feature code and rights evidence, with hashes (SCI-03).

  • Each feature block and listing has a status: vintage_backed, lag_conservative, prospective_only or unavailable; every window ends at the cutoff.

  • The initial blocks are lagged returns, trailing volatility and peak risk, reliable volume, declared market context, optional vintage-backed macro and missingness flags.

Negative

  • A future macro revision, today's constituents for a past universe, or a historical LLM extraction without contamination control is excluded or labelled prospective_only (SCI-03-AC01, SCI-G10).

  • Training or Anthropic sharing is blocked where only display is covered, and Yahoo history is not a training input while DATA-DEC-15 is open (SCI-03-AC07).

  • Missingness is never treated as zero exposure.

Recovery

  • A late correction creates a new manifest version; the old one stays for the models built on it.

  • A rights withdrawal marks dependent manifests and artefacts ineligible (DATA-AT-12).

PH04-S08 · Walk-forward folds with purge, embargo and train-only fitting

Positive

  • Folds are chronological, expanding-origin, on common UTC date blocks across assets, and every transform, imputer, selector and calibrator is fitted inside its training or calibration partition (SCI-05).

  • Purge removes any earlier label whose interval or outcome_known_at crosses a boundary; the embargo is the task horizon plus one eligible session, calendar-aware for month tasks.

  • The partition manifest records the fit, calibration and validation boundaries and every exclusion; the trial registry records every configuration, seed, failed job and test access.

  • Campaigns stay within two a month across dev and prod, each at most 96 fits and six training-instance hours (POLICY-008), as capped SageMaker CPU jobs (ARC-04).

Negative

  • A random split, a full-dataset scaler, a test-selected model or a fixed 20-row gap for a 12-month label is rejected (SCI-05-AC01).

  • Every learned fit, head, seed and ablation counts toward the one project cap (SCI-05-AC03).

  • A final test that was opened and failed is spent and cannot be used for selection again (SCI-05-AC04).

Recovery

  • An interrupted training job is reconciled by its job identity, never resubmitted blindly.

  • A campaign that would exceed its fits, hours or admitted budget aborts and records why.

PH04-S09 · Proper scores, uncertainty and the evaluation report

Positive

  • Events use Brier as primary and log loss as secondary; distributions use pinball over q10, q50 and q90, central-80% coverage, width and median error; reliability shows counts (SCI-06).

  • Paired candidate-minus-baseline losses on matched origins, with 2,000 moving-block bootstrap replicates of whole date blocks at least the label horizon long; fewer than 20 non-overlapping blocks labels the evidence exploratory.

  • Reports show hit rate alongside calibration, error, coverage, turnover and the benchmark, per market, kind, horizon and regime, with abstention coverage.

Negative

  • A score over issued forecasts only, without coverage, is rejected (SCI-06-AC05).

  • Extraction quality never counts as predictive value (SCI-06-AC08).

  • A secondary metric never replaces the declared primary afterwards.

Recovery

  • A failed or partial evaluation is reported incomplete, never green.

  • Rescoring after an outcome amendment creates new score versions beside the old.

PH04-S10 · Regime and path-risk module

Positive

  • The descriptive regime uses the sign of the trailing 60-session return and 20-session log-return volatility against an 80th-percentile threshold fitted only in the training partition (F3-REG-v1).

  • Within-horizon risk events (drawdown or adverse excursion beyond a threshold) use separately fitted and calibrated estimators, never the terminal distribution.

  • Regimes serve as strata and features, with past-only smoothing.

Negative

  • Missing windows give no regime, and NAV volatility is not annualised with a fixed 252.

  • A regime is never presented as a probability or a causal market state.

  • An available return forecast never implies an available path-risk probability.

Recovery

  • A threshold refitted in a later fold is a new version; earlier outputs keep theirs.

  • When path-risk inputs are incomplete, the path-risk output abstains while the return output may still issue.

PH04-S11 · Similar-days search since 2015

Positive

  • Candidates come from every eligible session since 1 January 2015, at most 20 neighbours on training-standardised trailing 20 and 60-session returns, 20-session volatility and 60-session drawdown, with deterministic tie-breaks (SCI-OA-02).

  • A candidate is admitted only if its full label interval matured and its outcome was known before the current cutoff; inside a fold it stays within the fit region.

  • Overlapping windows are grouped per economic entity and the effective evidence is shown.

  • Where a listing's history starts later, the search uses what there is and states its start date and candidate count.

Negative

  • An immature analog is excluded however close it is (SCI-04-AC02).

  • A short history is never padded from another listing, nor extended from Yahoo history without the owner's answer to DATA-DEC-15.

  • No neighbour or event count is presented as proof of an effect.

Recovery

  • A rerun with the same manifest returns the same neighbours.

  • A backfill that extends history produces a new feature version; earlier results keep theirs.

PH04-S12 · Event studies and event factors for all seven kinds

Positive

  • The first wave covers all seven kinds for the US, UK and India; each event is dated by when it became known and its source's rights are checked (SCI-OA-01).

  • Scheduled kinds may use the prerelease mode only where the schedule was public by the cutoff; unscheduled kinds enter only in postrelease mode at a later daily origin.

  • A surprise is measured against an expectation known by the cutoff, or else the announcement alone, labelled as such.

  • Numerical code computes event counts, spread and intervals, accounting for overlapping outcomes and multiple testing; each kind declares its windows and comparators before its campaign.

Negative

  • No kind informs a forecast before it passes its own qualification, staged inside POLICY-008's ceiling.

  • A retrospective event study is descriptive and never qualifies a predictive feature; release content never leaks into a prior-close predictor.

  • Sparse or biased event data, or an event with no point-in-time date, gives low_evidence or unavailable, never a confident effect.

Recovery

  • A corrected event record appends a new version, and affected factors are rebuilt as new versions.

  • On a day with no extraction, the factor is missing, never zero sentiment; only a separately qualified no-news variant may run.

PH04-S13 · Regularised and boosted challengers with calibration

Positive

  • R1 is ridge for terminal return and logistic per binary event; R2 is bounded histogram gradient-boosted mean, quantile and event models; each artefact is keyed by task, horizon, currency, pool, features, fold, cutoff, calibrator and schema (SCI-04).

  • A sigmoid calibrator is fitted on the separate chronological calibration block after estimator selection and frozen with the artefact; isotonic or conformal methods need their own budgeted campaign and demonstrated time-series coverage.

  • Each output records its main drivers.

  • Training runs as capped SageMaker CPU jobs, and approved artefacts serve from Lambda only where the fit is shown (ARC-04).

Negative

  • Crossing quantiles are rejected, or corrected only by an approved monotone mapping fitted without test access.

  • There is no model per ticker by default; unsupported tasks stay visible as baseline-only or unavailable.

  • Nothing here authorises a training call or a promotion.

Recovery

  • An artefact that does not fit the Lambda envelope is not deployed; a costed serving alternative goes to the owner.

  • A failed training job is reconciled by job identity and its fits still count.

PH04-S14 · Ablation ladder and incremental value of each feature block

Positive

  • Ablations run rules and statistics, then numerical models, then LLM-structured factors, then approved family combinations, with patterns and analog features, on the same dates, task and folds (ARC-05).

  • Incremental value covers proper score, calibration, stability, missing-source resilience, latency, rights and marginal cash cost.

  • LLM factors are prospective_only unless a bounded contamination study supports more.

Negative

  • A block that only improves extraction language does not qualify (SCI-06-AC08).

  • A model that depends on news cannot substitute zero sentiment when news is missing.

  • Every ablation spends the same fit cap.

Recovery

  • An aborted ablation is recorded as aborted with its fits counted.

  • When a factor source becomes ineligible, ablations that relied on it are marked ineligible, with their results kept.

PH04-S15 · Causal pattern detectors

Positive

  • Two detectors: a crossing of the trailing 20 and 60-session means, and a close above the prior 20-session maximum or below its minimum, confirmed on completed bars only and timestamped at their last input (PAT-MA-20-60, PAT-RANGE-20).

  • Each detector has hand-marked examples and a stated confirmation lag; detector correctness and predictive value are tested separately.

  • Candle extensions stay retained requirements, inactive.

Negative

  • No candle pattern runs on a NAV series, and no detector uses future-confirmed extremes (SCI-04-AC01).

  • Parameter alternatives count as trials, with false-discovery control across them.

  • A detector with no out-of-sample evidence is never shown as predictive.

Recovery

  • A detector fix is a new version; outputs from the old version stay readable.

  • Missing bars make the detector ineligible for that listing and date rather than guessing.

PH04-S16 · Contribution policy experiments

Positive

  • F7 compares owner-approved hypothetical policies with doing nothing, fixed-date benchmark contributions and a fixed-allocation policy, matching flow dates, currency, starting value, dividends, fees and slippage assumptions; gross and cost-adjusted results are shown separately (SCI-06).

  • Protected positions and bands are respected, and results compare downside improvement against lost growth.

  • A policy is shown live, labelled experimental, only after it beats the simple rules for 8 weeks in replay and shadow and the owner says yes (SCI-OA-09).

  • The owner's decision journal records what the owner decided, separately from the simulation.

Negative

  • No broker credential, order path or executable instruction exists; a simulation is never a trade or advice.

  • No tax treatment is assumed.

  • A policy that has not beaten the rules is not shown live.

Recovery

  • If a live policy's inputs become ineligible, it shows unavailable with the reason and its past results stay.

  • A replay interrupted part-way is rerun from its manifest and produces the same result.

PH04-S17 · Append-only scores and track-record summaries

Positive

  • Each Score names the outcome revision it used; original and corrected scores both survive with policy-labelled aggregates.

  • Track records by horizon, holding, model and regime, and 30, 90 and 365-day summaries, show sample size, sufficiency and censoring, with immature forecasts counted as pending.

  • Score evidence is kept for the account's lifetime (POLICY-010), apart from operational logs (14 days) and run summaries (90 days).

Negative

  • An unsuccessful forecast is never deleted or overwritten, and nothing is scored before maturity.

  • A summary with too few matured forecasts says so instead of showing a rate.

  • Hit rate never appears without calibration, error and coverage beside it.

Recovery

  • Summaries rebuilt from stored scores reproduce exactly.

  • A restore never resurrects deleted content, and an allowed record keeps the evidence that its source was removed.

PH04-S18 · Eight-week shadow trial and owner promotion

Positive

  • A candidate runs in shadow, writing immutable prospective predictions with no action authority, for 8 scored weeks, and must beat both the base rate and the current rules (SCI-OA-04).

  • A week that cannot be scored (a data outage, a holiday week) pauses the count; for 3, 6 and 12 months the 8 weeks check clean running and calibration on what has matured, and walk-forward history carries the accuracy evidence.

  • The owner's approval binds the exact artefact, calibrator, tasks, scope, evidence hash, limits, expiry and previous safe version; states run candidate, offline_qualified, shadow, owner_approved, probation, active, with rejected, paused and rolled_back.

  • A rules change takes the same trial against the current rules.

Negative

  • A tuner or agent can create a candidate but never promote one.

  • Fewer than 8 scored weeks, or failing either comparator, cannot be promoted; 8 weeks alone never validates 12-month accuracy.

  • An approval for a different artefact, an expired approval or a changed evidence hash is refused at execution.

Recovery

  • A promotion interrupted part-way is checked again against its approval and either completes once or stays unpromoted.

  • A rejected candidate stays readable with its evidence.

PH04-S19 · Probation, automatic revert on slipping and safety pause

Positive

  • A newly promoted model is on probation for 4 live weeks and reverts after 2 bad weeks; after that, 4 weeks running worse than the previous approved model on matured forecasts reverts it automatically (SCI-OA-05, SCI-OA-06).

  • A revert appends PromotionRecords (the slipping model rolled_back, the previous model active under its own unexpired approval) and notifies the owner.

  • An advance-approved safety trigger pauses only the affected scope and notifies the owner; invalid outputs stay withheld (POLICY-003).

  • The previous approved model keeps issuing shadow forecasts, so comparisons are paired.

Negative

  • If the previous model is ineligible (revoked rights, expired approval, invalid hash), the scope pauses instead of reverting.

  • Switching forward again, or restarting a paused scope, needs the owner's yes; nothing restarts itself.

  • A week with no matured paired forecasts neither counts toward nor breaks a run.

Recovery

  • A revert interrupted part-way completes idempotently from its records.

  • A failed notification is retried; the revert or pause stands meanwhile.

PH04-S20 · Bounded self-correction overlay

Positive

  • Code grades every matured forecast daily and may shift a forecast's probabilities by at most 5 percentage points a week and a signal's weight by at most 10% a month (SCI-OA-03).

  • Each adjustment is an append-only, versioned overlay on the frozen calibrator and weights, named in every Prediction's calibrator_version, with its provenance persisted before any Prediction names it.

  • An adjustment rolls back automatically if scores worsen against the unadjusted version on later matured paired forecasts.

Negative

  • Anything outside a band waits for the owner; the overlay never changes learned parameters, features, rules, task definitions, the bands or the 35/40/25 weights.

  • An overlay fitted on any outcome known after the origin's cutoff is rejected.

  • No Claude or research agent can make or approve an adjustment.

Recovery

  • A failed overlay step leaves the previous overlay in force.

  • A rollback restores the previous overlay version and keeps the full history.
