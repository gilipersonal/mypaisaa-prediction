Proposed acceptance

L4-S01 · Forecast tasks, calendars and mature labels

  • Define next trading day,5/20 trading days and3/6/12months: target, total/price/excess return, currency, calendar target date, maturity and correction policy.

  • Preserve3–5-year assumption-based scenarios separately; no score or maturity label implies future outcomes have already occurred.

  • Each prediction pins as-of/cutoff/task/horizon/model/calibration/data/code and probability or interval interpretation.

L4-S02 · Transparent rule and statistical baseline

  • Specify the retained indicators/pillars, seeds/lookbacks and macro-basket completeness from forecasting-scope; NAV series do not receive fake candle features.

  • Implement only after approval: known-answer rules plus naïve/trend and reproducible bootstrap/linear baselines with explicit uncertainty meaning.

  • Distinguish rule strength from calibrated probabilities; preserve disabled/insufficient/no-clear-view states.

L4-S03 · Walk-forward backtest and leakage controls

  • Use temporal walk-forward folds with horizon-aware purge/embargo and train-only transforms/calibration; deliberately leaky fixtures must fail.

  • Record dataset hashes, code/feature/model versions, split dates, fees/spreads/income/FX, survivorship and history limits.

  • Report calibration/error/coverage/turnover/growth/drawdown/sample intervals and benchmarks; decide objective trade-offs and evidence thresholds before promotion.

L4-S04 · Risk regimes, analogs and event studies

  • Preserve F3 regimes/volatility/downside and F4 analog/event modules with past-only smoothing, horizon-aware neighbour exclusion and uncertainty.

  • Numerical code computes event count/spread/interval; overlapping outcomes and multiple testing are accounted for.

  • Sparse/biased event data and missing point-in-time events yield low-evidence/unavailable states rather than a confident effect.

L4-S05 · Directional and distribution challengers

  • Preserve F5 tree/gradient challenger, direction/quantiles/calibration and interpretable drivers; conformal labels require demonstrated time-series coverage.

  • Run R0 rules/statistical→M1 numerical→M2 LLM-structured features→M3 approved family ablations, measuring incremental cost and evidence.

  • Choose A/B execution via PH00 ADR; no training calls or artefact promotion authorised by this story draft.

L4-S06 · Pattern detectors and contribution experiments

  • Retain F8 candle/chart/technical detectors with hand-marked examples, confirmation lag, per-kind eligibility and false-discovery/out-of-sample controls.

  • Retain F7 contribution/rebalance scenarios, protected positions/bands and fees, comparing downside improvement against lost growth.

  • Scenario assumptions and owner decision journal remain explicit; no broker credential or executable order exists.

L4-S07 · Immutable forecast/outcome scorecards

  • Append forecasts, revisions, mature outcomes and corrections; never overwrite unsuccessful predictions or score before maturity.

  • Show proof/eligibility, horizon/holding/model/regime track record and30/90/365-day summaries with sample sufficiency and censoring.

  • Durable audit and raw score evidence survive operational log expiry.

L4-S08 · Challenger shadow, owner promotion and rollback

  • Require baseline/mature-outcome evidence then shadow observation and explicit owner approval for every live model/calibration/strategy change.

  • Agree per-horizon metric thresholds, effectively independent sample sufficiency, shadow duration, subgroup gates and probation; eight weeks does not validate 12 months.

  • Pin previous compatible model/data/feature/calibration pointers; failed or drifted candidate stays disabled and rollback preserves audit.
