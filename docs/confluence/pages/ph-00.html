PH00-S01 · Trace every requirement and owner decision to a ready story

Positive

  • A RequirementTrace file maps each of CAP-01 to CAP-19 and DEC-001 to DEC-041 to at least one ready story (local id and Jira key), or to a recorded reason: superseded by a named owner answer, or out of scope in the owner's own words.

  • Every owner answer in the decision log, POLICY-001 to POLICY-012 and the ARC-06b card is traced to the stories whose acceptance carries it.

  • The trace keeps what is implemented today (the FastAPI health route, the sample SSE stream and CI) apart from planned capabilities, and marks real holdings and documents as deferred.

  • A check script fails when a requirement, decision or owner answer has no story and no reason, and passes on the complete trace.

Negative

  • A local draft, a dropped story or a story without a Jira key never counts as covering a requirement.

  • The superseded layered-development map of 1 November 2026 and MA pages are cited only as provenance, never as the current trace.

Recovery

  • When a story is later split, merged or dropped, the check fails until the trace names its replacement or a reason; earlier trace versions stay in git history.

PH00-S02 · Supersede the historical ADRs and record the selected directions as ADRs

Positive

  • ADR 0006 (single Lightsail host, PostgreSQL, the £50 AWS-only budget) is marked Superseded, naming the answers that replaced it: ARC-02 DynamoDB and S3, ARC-11 separate dev and prod accounts, and the £100 all-in ceiling; its text stays as history.

  • ADR 0005's use of the Claude Agent SDK is marked superseded by ARC-06 (the Anthropic client SDK in an explicit loop, one Lambda worker per route), keeping its bounded-agent rules.

  • ADR 0001 to 0004 are each checked against the twelve directions as refined and marked Accepted, Amended (with the amendment written in) or Superseded.

  • New ADRs record ARC-02 (one controlled writer), ARC-03 (Lambda Durable Functions), Terraform only with its S3 state backend and bootstrap (owner, 3 November 2026), network N0 with the cost-guard SCP, and SSM Parameter Store SecureString for secrets; each links its decision log entry.

Negative

  • No ADR changes or reinterprets an owner answer; where an ADR and the decision log differ, the ADR is the record that changes.

  • No AWS resource, account, purchase or apply follows from writing an ADR.

Recovery

  • If a later owner answer reverses a direction, a new ADR supersedes the old one; no ADR is deleted or rewritten in place.

PH00-S03 · Set versioning, compatibility and ownership rules for every shared contract

Positive

  • A contract register lists every contract in the foundation schemas and the phase contract catalogues with its producer, consumers, the one writer, current version and status (specified or planned).

  • Versioning rules are written down: an added optional field is a minor version; a removed field or a changed meaning is a major version that needs a migration and a period where both versions are read; every stored record carries the schema version it was written with.

  • One error shape is shared by every API response: a code, a message, the request_id and the trace_id, with no secrets or holdings (MP-36 proposal).

  • Every record that affects money, forecasts or approvals links the schema, data and code versions that produced it and states that it carries no trading authority.

  • Compatibility cases run offline: an old reader on a new minor version passes, a new reader on an old record passes, and a meaning change without a major version fails.

Negative

  • The check refuses a contract change that alters a field's meaning without a major version and a migration.

  • No contract defines a field for broker credentials, an order or a trade instruction.

Recovery

  • A failed migration leaves the previous version readable; a correction is a new version that supersedes the old record, which is kept (ADR 0002).

PH00-S04 · Map the synthetic fixtures to the cases they prove and register real samples for later

Positive

  • A coverage matrix lists every synthetic pack (the real-ID instruments and the PDF, PNG, expected-import and operations companions for local and dev) against the import, ledger and forecast cases it exercises, with prices, accounts, transactions and outcomes marked synthetic.

  • The matrix names the missing cases: Excel workbooks and malformed, blurred, rotated, password-protected and hostile documents (embedded instructions, oversized files), each assigned to the PH-01 story that will add it.

  • A deferred real-sample register lists the real statement layouts the owner may supply later, each with its intake route and the check it must pass before real use; no planning exit needs a real sample.

Negative

  • No fixture result is reported as evidence of parser robustness or forecast accuracy.

  • No real holding, account number or statement enters the repo, a fixture or the dev account.

Recovery

  • A real sample offered before its intake story is built is not stored anywhere by MP; the register records it as waiting and the owner keeps the file.

PH00-S05 · Design the owner's product journeys and every screen state for web, iPhone and iPad

Positive

  • Journeys cover the home screen with my holdings first and the top 5 per segment below (owner, 3 November 2026, MP-177) (US large caps, US tech, India Nifty 50, India midcap, one ETF list each for the US, UK and India, gold and silver), holdings and imports kept separate, per-horizon forecasts with their proof, research and chat, the action journal, cost visible from the main dashboard, owner approvals, and privacy and recovery.

  • Every screen shows its loading, empty, error, stale, offline, no-model, disagreement and abstention states, and never hides risk, staleness or a provider's status behind a spinner.

  • At least two options per journey are made locally with mp-design-first, synced to private Claude HTML artifacts and recorded in docs/design/artifacts.json; the owner keeps both options on all eight journeys, with a flip between them (owner, 4 November 2026, MP-178). A flip made once anywhere in a journey opens every screen of that journey in that view on that device, and in the app each iPhone, iPad and browser keeps its own choice. OWN-01a, 5 November 2026 (MP-184): the owner picked "Whole journey" (by the owner's words in chat, "for the remaining pick up recommended options"). OWN-01b, 5 November 2026 (MP-184): the owner picked "Your real devices only" (by the owner's words in chat, "for the remaining pick up recommended options").

  • Layouts adapt across web, iPhone and iPad and meet WCAG 2.2 AA (owner, 3 November 2026, MP-177). The Apple app lays out by window width, so a narrow iPad window uses the phone layout. MP40-Q12b, 5 November 2026 (MP-184): the owner picked "Any window width" (pressed on the page).

  • The SvelteKit version and the device list are checked against current official releases before any package is named, and nothing is described as installed.

Negative

  • No screen offers a trade, an order or personalised advice; ranked actions are labelled decision support.

  • No screen shows a combined score when one of its three components is missing (POLICY-006).

  • No UI code is written, and no design is shared beyond the owner's private artifacts without a separate yes.

Recovery

  • If the owner rejects every option for a journey, a new options round is made for that journey alone; chosen journeys are kept.

PH00-S06 · Design every admin journey before the first build

Positive

  • Journeys for Files and Imports, Audit, Runs and Health, Agents, Models and Evaluations, Approvals and Configuration, Costs and Budgets, Incidents and Recovery, and Identity and Devices.

  • Each journey covers investigation and comparison, approve and reject, source inspection, reprocessing and correction, configuration changes, cost reconciliation, the stale, conflicting, expired and denied cases, and failure, replay, restore, rollback and deletion.

  • A permission inventory lists every admin action, whether it needs step-up (ARC-07) and what it writes to the audit.

  • Cost monitoring stays reachable from the main app as well as from admin.

  • Options are made locally, synced to private Claude HTML artifacts and recorded; the owner picks one per journey.

Negative

  • No admin action edits the ledger outside the controlled writer, promotes a model without its evidence, or deletes evidence without a recorded tombstone.

  • No household or multi-user administration is designed; MP has one owner.

Recovery

  • A rejected journey gets its own new options round; chosen journeys are kept.

PH00-S07 · Record the owner's whole-product UX approval and the separate permission to build

Positive

  • A DecisionRecord holds the owner's words approving the complete UX (product and admin journeys) at an exact commit, with the option kept per journey, or both with a flip (owner, 4 November 2026, MP-178), and each design's artifact URL and sha256.

  • Permission to build is a separate DecisionRecord naming the phases that may start; without it every story stays implementation_authorised false.

  • The record lists every item still open at approval (owner questions and gaps), each with who decides it.

Negative

  • A phase draft, a fixture pass, a Jira status, silence or a default selection never counts as approval.

  • UX approval is never read as permission to build, to apply Terraform or to spend.

Recovery

  • If a design changes after approval, the approval is marked stale for the affected journeys and the owner is asked again; earlier records are kept.
