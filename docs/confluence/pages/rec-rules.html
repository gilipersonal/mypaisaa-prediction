Numbers from code and models

Ledger figures, returns, XIRR and risk are Python. Forecasts come from evaluated numerical models. Claude explains, and every figure it quotes comes from a versioned tool result.

Frozen, then graded

Every forecast records its cutoff, model, horizon and distribution. It is scored only after the horizon and never rewritten.

Nothing changes without you

One controlled writer commits only the exact change the owner approved. A better model goes live only with the owner's approval and its evidence.

It cannot trade

No broker credentials, no order path, no personalised financial advice. Decision support with its uncertainty on show.

WhatHow

Holdings

One record of what you own, in GBP, USD and INR

CSV, Excel, PDFs, screenshots and manual entry; uncertain values stay drafts

Coverage

20 to 30 shares and ETFs across the US, UK and India

Yahoo Finance and Twelve Data Grow, each listing checked for rights

Forecasts

Ranges for 1 day, 5, 10, 20 trading days, 3, 6, 12 months

Baselines first, then bounded challengers, qualified per task

Scenarios

3 and 5-year downside, central and upside

Editable assumptions that need approval; no implied likelihood

Monitoring

Quote and risk checks every 30 minutes; a 07:30 briefing on weekdays (MP40-Q11b "Weekdays only")

Push to the app and the PWA; quiet hours 22:00 to 07:00. Critical covers security and service faults only, so investment alerts wait for 07:30 (MP40-Q11a "Your lines, no investment wake-ups")

Recommendations

Add, hold, pause, trim, rebalance

With reasons and uncertainty; MP abstains when the evidence is thin. No label appears until evaluation supports the thresholds and you approve a ranking policy version (OWN-09 "Evidence first")
