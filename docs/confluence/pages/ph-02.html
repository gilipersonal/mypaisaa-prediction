Proposed acceptance

L2-S01 · Cognito identity and owner recovery

  • Specify managed Apple/Google/passkey sign-in and server-verified account linking; email similarity alone cannot merge identities.

  • Define two independent passkey paths, verified provider links, lost-device/recovery proof, session revocation and sensitive-action reauthentication.

  • Demonstrate denied cross-environment/cross-owner access and audit recovery/link changes without exposing identity tokens.

L2-S02 · Private durable storage and job contracts

  • Specify isolated dev/prod identities and encrypted storage with least privilege; synthetic dev cannot write production.

  • Specify conditional ledger/audit/outbox writes and S3 hash/manifest publication boundaries; uploaded bytes and database pointers are not one transaction.

  • Retries use idempotency keys, bounded backoff and quarantine; no duplicate financial side effect after timeout/replay.

L2-S03 · Launch dashboard cost meter and reconciliation

  • Main dashboard shows dev/prod/local API/shared-unallocated costs with provider/agent/model/feature/service drill-down and source age.

  • Separate live estimates from delayed billing and gross usage from credit applied/cash; Anthropic billing collection every 6 hours, AWS daily, bounded and cached.

  • Unknown shared credit stays unknown and is never multiplied per environment; show collector failures and reconciliation discrepancies.

L2-S04 · Enforce discretionary spending admission

  • Evaluate forecast monthly cash against £60 warning/£75 reduced optional work/£85 pause-new-optional-paid thresholds while retaining £100 total ceiling and £70+£30 plan.

  • Use bounded reservations/limits for concurrent jobs; refuse uncovered or unverifiable Claude calls and pause optional calls at agreed low-credit conditions without paid fallback.

  • Show uncertainty from delayed AWS billing; approval thresholds are not claimed to provide an instantaneous universal AWS shutoff. Keep deterministic essential views available.

L2-S05 · Authenticated API and resumable SSE

  • Version authenticated APIs and short-lived heartbeat-enabled SSE; use durable event IDs/cursor recovery where supported, refetch durable records after gaps.

  • Define reconnect/token-expiry/ordering/dedup/backpressure and multi-tab/background rules; no secrets or durable financial truth solely in a stream.

  • Cost active connections and validate selected API/Lambda streaming compatibility before implementation; starter unauthenticated volatile SSE is not production evidence.

L2-S06 · Telemetry, evidence separation and original archive

  • Plan logs 14 days, rich traces 30, run summaries 90 subject to measured cost; maintain distinct durable financial/forecast/approval/source audit lifecycles.

  • Plan encrypted private S3 originals with renewable 24-month retention and owner extension/deletion controls; define versions/derivatives/backups and no automatic expiry deletion yet.

  • Redact document contents, holdings, tokens and prompts from operational telemetry; correlation IDs link to access-controlled evidence.
