Proposed acceptance

L7-S01 · Native SwiftUI within React Native shell

  • Swift owns native views,persistence,widgets and secure-device integration; RN shell owns navigation/bridge contracts.

  • Deliver approved adaptive dashboard,holdings,imports,forecast/proof,chat,cost and approval journeys across iPhone/iPad/Duo matrix.

  • Test shared API/event and shell/native schema compatibility and discoverable error/recovery states.

L7-S02 · Offline cache, privacy and accessibility

  • Encrypt/cache only approved data,display per-record age,refetch after reconnect and revoke access/session caches appropriately.

  • VoiceOver,Dynamic Type,reduced motion,focus/chart alternatives and large/adaptive layouts satisfy agreed acceptance.

  • No confidential holdings appear on lockscreen,app snapshots or exported share content by default without approved settings.

L7-S03 · PWA/native push and permission fallbacks

  • Implement first-version app/chat,native and PWA push per verified OS/browser capability and user permission.

  • Use durable outbox,dedup/deep links,quiet hours and neutral payloads; denied/background-disabled delivery remains visible in-app.

  • Measure delivery limitations; do not promise instant OS delivery or infer no risk from silence.

L7-S04 · Native capability backlog without hidden scope loss

  • Specify widgets,share-sheet intake,search/deep links,Siri/AppIntents and long-job Live Activities with privacy,availability and cost constraints.

  • Classify each capability launch-required or later activation by explicit owner choice; no silent removal of inherited scope.

  • Keep Android product screens outside current agreed release scope; RN shell does not imply an Android UI.
