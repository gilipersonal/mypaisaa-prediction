Proposed acceptance

L1-S01 · Owner-scoped accounts and listing identities

  • Stable security/share-class and listing identities distinguish venue, trading currency and quote units; aliases are effective-dated.

  • ISIN/name-only ambiguity produces candidate matches for owner confirmation, never a guessed listing.

  • Owner/environment scope is enforced at every read/write and account mapping; fixture IDs remain real and private actual holdings stay deferred.

  • Account/wrapper labels retain source context without implementing unverified tax rules.

L1-S02 · Private validated upload intake

  • Byte limits, magic type, checksum, archive/page/pixel/runtime checks and quarantine status precede extraction; exact limit values need approval.

  • Original bytes/version and an upload record exist before processing; an object-created notification cannot mark a ledger import committed.

  • Repeated/out-of-order upload notifications and orphaned objects reconcile idempotently; rejected uploads have a visible reason.

  • Protected originals remain private; upload authorization is scoped/short-lived and app-side single-use semantics are enforced rather than assumed from a signed URL.

L1-S03 · Versioned extraction for every agreed method

  • Deterministic parsers handle supported layouts; document/image LLM extraction is bounded, tool-free and produces validated typed proposals with source locations.

  • Every field records raw/normalized values, parser/model/schema version and page/sheet/row/cell evidence; missing data remains missing.

  • Cells/macros/formulas/links and document instructions are untrusted; no script/formula/network execution is needed to import a workbook.

  • Generic fixtures are distinguished from validated broker layouts; encrypted PDFs are unlocked only through the approved secure local flow, never a logged/model password.

L1-S04 · Instrument and account matching

  • Matching uses verified identifiers plus venue/currency/share-class context and preserves confidence/evidence.

  • Unknown/ambiguous matches block only dependent rows until explicit mapping; choosing a mapping creates a new draft revision.

  • A factsheet alone can describe a candidate instrument but cannot invent owner units, cost or purchase history.

  • A mapping/template reused later is versioned and scoped; it cannot silently turn one listing into another because a ticker or name looks similar.

L1-S05 · Exact normalization of money, dates and events

  • Finite decimal strings and explicit units are used; GBX converts to GBP exactly once, and unknown fees/currencies are not silently zero/defaulted.

  • Source booked net amounts, unit prices, fees/withholding and FX execution/reference rates remain distinguishable and reconcile under an approved tolerance.

  • Trade/effective, settlement, observed/available and recorded times have explicit meanings; date-only input is not invented into a precise UTC instant.

  • Each supported event has validated cash/quantity legs; transfers/FX/splits preserve economic boundaries and never sum unlike units.

L1-S06 · Preview, guided edits and explicit subset selection

  • Preview shows every accepted/blocked/duplicate/excluded row, account and instrument mapping, before/after values, errors and metric eligibility.

  • An edit/subset/mapping/parser change creates a new canonical draft hash and invalidates earlier approval.

  • The default partial-file policy remains an owner choice; any approved subset is independently consistent, explicit and traceable.

  • Confirm/Edit/Cancel/resume/stale/expired states have accessible web/native acceptance scenarios; document content cannot self-approve.

L1-S07 · Business deduplication and replay-safe identity

  • File identity, source row identity and economic-event identity are separate, scoped and versioned.

  • Stable provider/account transaction references suppress true replays; two genuine same-date/same-amount trades with distinct IDs remain two events.

  • Without sufficient reference evidence, a near/exact-content collision is reviewed rather than automatically discarded or inserted.

  • Reprocessing with a new parser does not bypass economic dedup; a changed fact becomes a correction proposal, not a second original trade.

L1-S08 · Owner approval and atomic visible commit

  • Approval binds owner/environment, canonical selected rows, draft hash and expected ledger revision with server-verified authentication and expiry.

  • Concurrent/stale/expired/wrong-scope requests refuse mutation; an ambiguous timeout resolves through the existing durable receipt.

  • A successful logical commit couples financial changes, required audit and outbox obligations; staged work is invisible to all consumers until a valid activation marker.

  • Large workbooks use a proven bounded/fenced staging design or another approved atomic-visibility strategy; transaction item/byte limits include metadata/audit and cannot be treated as row counts.

L1-S09 · Deterministic ledger replay and versioned projections

  • Rebuilding from the same committed events/policy versions produces identical projections; caches are not financial truth.

  • Trade/settlement and transfer ordering is explicit; unresolved ties or unsupported margin/short behaviour are surfaced rather than guessed.

  • Failed/staged/superseded work cannot leak into active holdings, metrics, agent context or read APIs.

  • A later complete history reconciles with an opening observation and activates a new baseline/window, never adds the same units twice.

L1-S10 · Cash, units and valuation reconciliation

  • Cash/quantity/fees/FX/unit/date/coverage differences are classified with source and derived values and an approved tolerance.

  • An unexplained difference cannot be cleared by an invented plug trade or silent overwrite.

  • Price and FX timestamps/availability/quality accompany totals; an unpriced holding leaves a partial total and visible missing coverage.

  • Owner resolution records the reason and source and creates a new revision when financial facts change.

L1-S11 · Corrections, reversal and baseline rebase

  • Corrections and import-level reversal are separate preview/approval operations linked to original events and sources.

  • Dependent later events and observations are assessed before reversal; a bulk delete cannot leave impossible quantities or remove audit history.

  • Recomputed views are versioned/restated while issued forecasts and their input revisions remain historically identifiable.

  • A repeated correction/reversal request returns the same result and does not reapply its effects.

L1-S12 · Incomplete holdings and eligible performance measures

  • Missing purchase history/fees/FX is represented as per-measure insufficiency, not fabricated transactions or a zero return.

  • Holding/account/portfolio XIRR uses its own defined flow boundary, dated signs, terminal valuation and FX convention; internal portfolio trades/dividends/transfers are not external contributions.

  • Solver failure, ambiguous multiple roots, same-day-only histories or incomplete terminal valuation return an explicit unavailable/ambiguous result with evidence.

  • Known-answer and invariant examples verify monetary residuals and rate tolerances with correct units; synthetic outcomes never prove predictive skill.

L1-S13 · Originals, audit, retention and recovery lineage

  • Every authoritative change carries durable source/actor/approval/commit links; required business lineage is unsampled and separate from short-lived operational telemetry.

  • Original retention is renewable24months with owner extension/deletion, without assuming automatic expiry or irreversible Object Lock.

  • A source-deletion request and backup restore cannot resurrect content; remaining evidence joins expose deleted/unavailable source state honestly.

  • Synthetic missing/altered-audit-event exercises are detected using the approved threat model/anchoring design; a hash chain alone is not described as tamper-proof.

L1-S14 · Scoped portfolio reads, progress and accessible clients

  • Paginated reads serve owner/account/instrument/date/status access patterns with version/cursor and quality metadata; no unbounded table scan is the default.

  • SSE reconnect/resume and durable GET recovery cannot lose commit truth or repeat a mutation; starter volatile SSE is not claimed production-ready.

  • Private values appear only in authorized app views; privacy mode, VoiceOver, responsive/iPad layout and offline/stale states are specified before implementation.

  • Read-only agents cite the same committed version/evidence; a notification or model summary cannot become an alternative ledger.

L1-S15 · Owner/environment isolation and bounded processing costs

  • API/worker/source-access/commit paths recheck owner/environment and prevent fixture loading into live data; no trading or broker execution credentials exist.

  • Signed source links, raw fields/prompts/passwords/account numbers and holdings values do not enter general telemetry or push previews.

  • Extraction/retry/queue work has bounded file/time/token/concurrency reservations and idempotent usage accounting; unknown credit pauses uncovered model calls.

  • Import activity links to the main per-env Claude/AWS cost dashboard; collectors remain cached/scheduled, with no paid billing call per UI refresh.

L1-S16 · Layer acceptance and later real-sample qualification

  • All declared L1 examples pass across local/dev with real identifiers and simulated observations; source originals/golden records remain intact.

  • Fault injection covers duplicate delivery, stale approvals, crash-midstage, ambiguous commit response, source tampering, cross-scope access and deletion-after-restore.

  • Generic PDF/PNG/CSV foundations are accurately labelled; missing Excel/real-broker/photographed-image cases remain visible until filled and tested.

  • Implementation readiness requires approved unresolved choices, source/data rights before live use, tested supported layouts, complete UX and trace from requirements to evidence; phase status or a diagram alone cannot certify completion.
