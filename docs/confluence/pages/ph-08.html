Proposed acceptance

L8-S01 · Release readiness and reconciled actual-input onboarding

  • Require approved full baseline/UX,per-layer acceptance and whole-product contracts/evidence; no Jira status proves release readiness.

  • Actual holdings/listings/documents may be added progressively; verify identity,source entitlements and named layout coverage before their real use, retaining unsupported review/manual paths.

  • No live claims or training accuracy derives from synthetic fixtures; no deployment/spend follows automatically from draft approval.

L8-S02 · Restore, rollback and monthly audit exercises

  • Agree RPO/RTO by record class,perform monthly focused exercises plus relevant storage/import/audit change checks.

  • Restore original versions,ledger,audit,outcomes,approval and model pointers into isolated recovery target and reconcile counts/hashes/financial balances.

  • Exercise rollback of app/contracts/model/calibration and failed import reversal; no destructive restore into production or automatic expiry deletion.

L8-S03 · Resilience, incidents and bounded self-recovery

  • Test queue/DLQ/replay limits,out-of-order conversation events,market outage,missed forecast,expired source access and stalled collector.

  • Self-recovery uses approved bounded actions only; no agent may edit infrastructure or buy a provider.

  • Correlate alert→incident→run→source/model evidence; avoid hidden lost events behind a healthy service health endpoint.

L8-S04 · Whole-release security, scientific and cost gates

  • Run relevant API/schema/ledger/scientific/no-leakage/agent/accessibility/native/offline/privacy/security gates before any separately approved independent review.

  • Demonstrate full launch usage and budget fallbacks within £100 aggregate,including dev/prod/evals/collectors/SSE/backups/builds and unverified/no credit cases.

  • Review blockers,non-blocking issues and rollback readiness; live model gate remains separate from application release approval.

L8-S05 · Owner operations and admin requirements

  • Capture admin requirements now: uploads/versions,ledger updates,runs/tools/models/evals,health,cost and approvals; owner designs the full portal later.

  • Ship required audit/owner controls in approved launch UX; later portal styling does not defer main-dashboard cost visibility or critical recovery controls.

  • Retain pause switches,incident escalation,monthly evidence review and documented owner procedures.
