## Users and purpose

Arun uses this existing owner-private Site to understand the proposed Mypaisaa Prediction system. The architecture atlas must make its end-to-end application story accessible to a non-specialist while exposing engineering contracts and integration points. It is documentation, not a product implementation or an investment dashboard.

## Evidence and constraints

The authority is the MP decision register, layered architecture, technology/network appendices and published MP Confluence planning hub. Initial regions of interest are the US, UK and India; wider markets are future extensions. The system is personal decision support, with no broker execution. Direct Anthropic client SDK, Lambda/S3/DynamoDB preference and a £100 all-in planning ceiling supersede older bootstrap assumptions. Unresolved technical choices stay visibly unresolved.

Preserve the existing Site ID, owner-private audience, concept preview and repository ownership. Do not call AWS, market-data or model APIs from this presentation, invent model performance, or expose actual holdings. Source links open their own authenticated services; this Site creates no app integrations or new permissions.

## Brand commitments

The owner explicitly selected a cinematic systems atlas with deep green/lime, large editorial headings, animated paths and guided chapters. Keep plain-language explanations alongside accurate engineering details and native Lucid AWS diagrams.

## Accessibility

The content is readable without animation. Motion is opt-in, stoppable and disabled by reduced-motion preference. Provide keyboard access, legible type, a mobile flow and text equivalents for diagrams and the Remotion story.
